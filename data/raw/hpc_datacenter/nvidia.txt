										<content:encoded><![CDATA[<div id="bsf_rt_marker"></div><p><span style="font-weight: 400;">The </span><span style="font-weight: 400;">Barcelona Supercomputing Center (BSC)</span><span style="font-weight: 400;">’s AI Factory Accelerator Programme, now in full operation following a pilot initiative, gives startups access to sovereign compute, technical support and training to support innovation across industries. </span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Many of these companies are also members of the </span><a target="_blank" href="https://www.nvidia.com/en-us/startups/"><span style="font-weight: 400;">NVIDIA Inception</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;"> program for cutting-edge startups — and as of today, access to some of the BSC AI Factory services are available through the NVIDIA Inception portal</span><span style="font-weight: 400;">. </span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">NVIDIA Inception complements the BSC AI Factory Accelerator Programme by providing members with training through </span><a target="_blank" href="https://www.nvidia.com/en-us/training/"><span style="font-weight: 400;">NVIDIA Deep Learning Institute</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;"> open courses, business mentoring, venture capital connections and other resources.</span></p>
<p><iframe title="Building Europe&amp;apos;s AI Stack — Inside the Barcelona Supercomputing Center" width="1200" height="675" src="https://www.youtube.com/embed/4GY-x4WAafc?feature=oembed" frameborder="0" allow="accelerometer; autoplay; clipboard-write; encrypted-media; gyroscope; picture-in-picture; web-share" referrerpolicy="strict-origin-when-cross-origin" allowfullscreen></iframe></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">An upcoming extension of MareNostrum 5 — BSC’s public infrastructure serving scientific and technological innovation, powered by NVIDIA accelerated computing — will deliver supercomputing access through the BSC AI Factory and other AI Factories across Europe. BSC AI Factory is among Europe’s first AI factories and the first in Spain, and has delivered more than 800 services to private and public-sector clients since its April 2025 launch.</span></p>
<div style="margin: 0 !important; padding: 0 !important; text-align: center; line-height: 1;"><a style="display: inline-flex; align-items: center; justify-content: center; margin: 0 !important; padding: 10px !important; background-color: #76b900; color: #ffffff !important; font-family: 'NVIDIA Sans', Arial, sans-serif; font-size: 16px; font-weight: bold; line-height: 1 !important; text-align: center; text-decoration: none !important; white-space: nowrap; vertical-align: top;" href="https://blogs.nvidia.com/gtc-berlin-2026-news/">Read All NVIDIA in Europe Announcements</a></div>
<p>&nbsp;</p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Three Inception members were recently named the BSC AI Factory Accelerator Programme’s AI Champions at the pilot initiative’s demo day:</span></p>
<p><b>Qatium</b><span style="font-weight: 400;">’s</span><span style="font-weight: 400;"> water-network platform — already used by more than 250 utilities in 16 countries — is now in early production with its Neural Water Intelligence technology, powered by proprietary AI models trained on MareNostrum 5. Built on NVIDIA accelerated computing, the </span><a target="_blank" href="https://github.com/rapidsai/cugraph"><span style="font-weight: 400;">NVIDIA cuGraph</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;"> library, the </span><a target="_blank" href="https://developer.nvidia.com/physicsnemo"><span style="font-weight: 400;">NVIDIA PhysicsNeMo</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;"> framework and </span><a target="_blank" href="https://www.nvidia.com/en-us/ai-data-science/foundation-models/nemotron/"><span style="font-weight: 400;">NVIDIA Nemotron</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;"> open models, the technology helps water utilities make better operational decisions every day.</span></p>
<p><iframe title="Qatium | Spain-Based Startups Advance Regional Innovation With Barcelona Supercomputing Center" width="1200" height="675" src="https://www.youtube.com/embed/lvf80-qr63E?feature=oembed" frameborder="0" allow="accelerometer; autoplay; clipboard-write; encrypted-media; gyroscope; picture-in-picture; web-share" referrerpolicy="strict-origin-when-cross-origin" allowfullscreen></iframe></p>
<p><b>OneCareAI</b><span style="font-weight: 400;">, a research spin-off from BSC, used </span><a target="_blank" href="https://www.nvidia.com/en-us/ai-data-science/products/nim-microservices/"><span style="font-weight: 400;">NVIDIA NIM</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;"> microservices to develop technology designed to identify signs of hidden atrial fibrillation — an irregular heart rhythm — in seemingly normal ECGs, helping clinicians identify patients who may be at risk of stroke before one occurs.</span></p>
<p><b>Avatia</b><span style="font-weight: 400;"> built a voice-first, multi-agent system that provides pharmaceutical companies with agentic coworkers that support sales representatives and give doctors transparent, curated information about medicines, ultimately helping the right treatment reach the right patient sooner. The system taps into NVIDIA NIM microservices, the </span><a target="_blank" href="https://www.nvidia.com/en-us/ai-data-science/foundation-models/nemotron/"><span style="font-weight: 400;">NVIDIA Nemotron 3</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;"> open model family and the open source </span><a target="_blank" href="https://github.com/NVIDIA-NeMo/Curator"><span style="font-weight: 400;">NVIDIA NeMo Curator</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;"> library.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Other BSC AI Factory startups — </span><span style="font-weight: 400;">Agrikola AI</span><span style="font-weight: 400;">, </span><span style="font-weight: 400;">Elna Health</span><span style="font-weight: 400;">, </span><span style="font-weight: 400;">Lokutor</span><span style="font-weight: 400;">, </span><span style="font-weight: 400;">Montevive AI</span><span style="font-weight: 400;">, </span><span style="font-weight: 400;">Oppy</span><span style="font-weight: 400;">, </span><span style="font-weight: 400;">Pack2Earth</span><span style="font-weight: 400;"> and </span><span style="font-weight: 400;">Up2Smart</span><span style="font-weight: 400;"> — span precision agriculture, medical imaging and knowledge access, speech services, enterprise security, sales intelligence and sustainable packaging.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">The cohort reflects the breadth of what advanced intelligence can now unlock: preventing strokes, detecting breast cancer subtypes before biopsy, putting medical knowledge at doctors’ fingertips faster, replacing petroleum-based plastic with biobased compostable materials, protecting crops with robots instead of chemicals and more.</span></p>
<p><i><span style="font-weight: 400;">Learn more about </span></i><a target="_blank" href="https://www.nvidia.com/en-us/startups/"><i><span style="font-weight: 400;">NVIDIA Inception</span></i></a><i><span style="font-weight: 400;"> and the </span></i><a target="_blank" href="https://bsc-aifactory.eu/accelerator/"><i><span style="font-weight: 400;">BSC AI Factory Accelerator Programme</span></i></a><i><span style="font-weight: 400;">.</span></i></p>

										<content:encoded><![CDATA[<div id="bsf_rt_marker"></div><p><span style="font-weight: 400;">In the cavernous hall of Kraftwerk, a former power station in Berlin, German Chancellor Friedrich Merz and NVIDIA founder and CEO Jensen Huang sat down to discuss Germany’s AI roadmap, and in particular, the industrial applications of AI.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Huang opened by tracing NVIDIA’s longstanding relationship with German industry, beginning with gaming systems, workstations and automotive design in the early 2000s and leading to today’s partnerships in autonomous vehicle development and digital twins for manufacturing.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">The conversation focused on how Germany can make the most of the AI revolution by playing to its strengths in manufacturing and automotive — and by encouraging AI adoption across the country’s ecosystem of small and medium-sized companies.</span></p>
<div style="margin: 0 !important; padding: 0 !important; text-align: center; line-height: 1;"><a style="display: inline-flex; align-items: center; justify-content: center; margin: 0 !important; padding: 10px !important; background-color: #76b900; color: #ffffff !important; font-family: 'NVIDIA Sans', Arial, sans-serif; font-size: 16px; font-weight: bold; line-height: 1 !important; text-align: center; text-decoration: none !important; white-space: nowrap; vertical-align: top;" href="https://blogs.nvidia.com/gtc-berlin-2026-news/">Read All NVIDIA in Europe Announcements</a></div>
<p>&nbsp;</p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Earlier in the day, as part of the Invest in Germany Summit, Chancellor Merz, the Federal Ministry for Economic Affairs and Energy and the Federal Ministry for Research, Technology and Space signed a Joint Declaration of Intent with NVIDIA.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">The German federal government’s aim is to merge traditional German engineering and manufacturing expertise with cutting-edge research and AI technology. The declaration sets out an agreement to work on a joint initiative with NVIDIA to advance cutting edge AI models for physical AI.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Formalizing and expanding NVIDIA’s collaboration with Germany’s federal government and its “Industry 4.0 Platform,” which is comprised of representatives from politics, industry, research and trade associations, helps achieve the government’s stated goal to “systematically bring industrial AI into widespread use and to establish a state-of-the-art industrial AI ecosystem in Europe.”</span></p>
<h2><b>Germany’s Ambition to Integrate AI With Industry</b></h2>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Merz and Huang dug into industrial AI and how NVIDIA can help Germany lead the way. The German Chancellor first laid out his government’s ambition.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">“We are fully committed to be one of the most advanced places in the world, not just in the European Union, but in the world to work with AI, to integrate AI into our industry,” Merz said. “I&#8217;m really optimistic that we will be one of the leading, if not the leading, country in the world in terms of the spread of AI for all the companies — our manufacturers, machinery, automotive industry and suppliers of the automotive industry. I think this is a great opportunity.”</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;"><img fetchpriority="high" decoding="async" class="aligncenter size-large wp-image-99107" src="https://blogs.nvidia.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/Merzquote-1680x672.jpg" alt="" width="1200" height="480" srcset="https://blogs.nvidia.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/Merzquote-1680x672.jpg 1680w, https://blogs.nvidia.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/Merzquote-960x384.jpg 960w, https://blogs.nvidia.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/Merzquote-1280x512.jpg 1280w, https://blogs.nvidia.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/Merzquote-1536x614.jpg 1536w, https://blogs.nvidia.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/Merzquote-scaled.jpg 2048w, https://blogs.nvidia.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/Merzquote-630x252.jpg 630w" sizes="(max-width: 1200px) 100vw, 1200px" />Merz called out the leaders of the small and medium businesses — the Mittelstand — in the audience. </span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">“The biggest advantage we have in Germany is our so-called Mittelstand,” Merz said. “Please open your doors for AI to make use of your data. This is a real secret and a real advantage for Germany to make use of all the data which is already available in our companies.”</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Huang agreed, and praised Germany’s world-class expertise in manufacturing companies both large and small, as well as its math and science excellence. That national expertise and knowledge is what sets the German economy apart, he said, and AI will only amplify it.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">“That knowledge belongs to you,” Huang said. “It cannot be replaced. AI can only augment your capability, it will not replace your capability. Take advantage of the technology. It’s a new instrument.”</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Germany’s expertise with physical technologies is a differentiator, </span><span style="font-weight: 400;">Huang and Merz agreed, and a powerful accelerator for the German economy when AI is included.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">“If we are successful in bringing these two aspects and parts of the business of the future together,” Merz said. “I’m really very optimistic that Germany can be one of the most successful economies in the world.”</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="aligncenter size-large wp-image-99108" src="https://blogs.nvidia.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/JHHMerzquote-1680x672.jpg" alt="" width="1200" height="480" srcset="https://blogs.nvidia.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/JHHMerzquote-1680x672.jpg 1680w, https://blogs.nvidia.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/JHHMerzquote-960x384.jpg 960w, https://blogs.nvidia.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/JHHMerzquote-1280x512.jpg 1280w, https://blogs.nvidia.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/JHHMerzquote-1536x614.jpg 1536w, https://blogs.nvidia.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/JHHMerzquote-scaled.jpg 2048w, https://blogs.nvidia.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/JHHMerzquote-630x252.jpg 630w" sizes="auto, (max-width: 1200px) 100vw, 1200px" />Huang described AI’s impact across virtually every industry as a new industrial revolution. </span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">“This industrial revolution is so mighty, so powerful,” Huang said. “In the Chancellor&#8217;s words, you are re-engineering Germany. This is your chance to do it well.”</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Merz envisioned a regular reunion with Huang to assess the progress Germany is making with AI.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">“I would like to see him regularly, let&#8217;s say at least once a year, and discuss the progress we made,” Merz said, “And see a guy sitting in my office being extremely happy about Germany as a place to invest.” </span></p>
<p><iframe loading="lazy" title="Fireside Talk: Chancellor Merz and Jensen Huang | Invest in Germany Summit 2026" width="1200" height="675" src="https://www.youtube.com/embed/PCroCEAGQ7g?start=1314&amp;feature=oembed" frameborder="0" allow="accelerometer; autoplay; clipboard-write; encrypted-media; gyroscope; picture-in-picture; web-share" referrerpolicy="strict-origin-when-cross-origin" allowfullscreen></iframe></p>
<h2><b>NVIDIA’s Investments in Germany</b></h2>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Huang also announced new investments in four German startups, part of a nearly $10 billion commitment by NVIDIA to support companies, universities, research institutions and more across Europe. </span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">NVIDIA has been investing across Germany’s startup ecosystem, supporting leading technology companies including </span><span style="font-weight: 400;">n8n, Neura Robotics</span><span style="font-weight: 400;"> and</span><span style="font-weight: 400;"> Black Forest Labs</span><span style="font-weight: 400;">. </span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Today, Huang announced new planned investments in</span><span style="font-weight: 400;"> Agile Robots, Avelios Medical, Mirelo</span><span style="font-weight: 400;"> and </span><span style="font-weight: 400;">Reflex Aerospace. </span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Agile Robots,</span><span style="font-weight: 400;"> a Munich-based robotics company, builds AI-powered robots, from robotic arms to its Agile ONE humanoid, for manufacturing, logistics and healthcare.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Avelios Medical,</span><span style="font-weight: 400;"> a Munich-based healthcare technology startup, develops an AI-native hospital information system that serves as the core system of record for clinical and administrative workflows, integrating AI directly into hospital operations to free up doctors and nurses to deliver better patient care.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Mirelo</span><span style="font-weight: 400;">, a German startup, develops AI models that generate sound effects, speech and music for video, helping creators and production teams produce high-quality audio that perfectly matches their visuals on screen.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Reflex Aerospace, </span><span style="font-weight: 400;">a Berlin-based space scaleup, designs and builds high-performance satellites ready for launch in about 12 months instead of years. </span></p>
<h2><b>Siemens and NVIDIA Advance Autonomous Manufacturing; Record-Breaking Quantum Simulation</b></h2>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Earlier in the day, Siemens announced it’s partnering with NVIDIA to advance AI-driven, autonomous factories. </span><span style="font-weight: 400;">Siemens <a href="https://blogs.nvidia.com/gtc-berlin-2026-news/#entry-postlet-siemens-and-nvidia-put-industrial-ai-to-work-in-erlangen">showcased new AI solutions at its Erlangen factory</a>, advancing its role as a global blueprint for autonomous manufacturing. Fully virtual production ramp-up reduces engineering effort by up to 85%, while agent-based production planning enables more adaptive manufacturing and cost savings of up to 20%.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">NVIDIA researchers used JUPITER, Europe’s largest supercomputer at the Jülich Supercomputing Centre, to run a </span><a target="_blank" href="https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/nvidia-runs-record-simulation-of-fault-tolerant-quantum-computing-at-julich-supercomputing-centre"><span style="font-weight: 400;">record-breaking simulation</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;"> of a fault-tolerant quantum computer. The 104-qubit simulation is the largest and most detailed of its kind to date.</span></p>

										<content:encoded><![CDATA[<div id="bsf_rt_marker"></div><p><span style="font-weight: 400;">At a creative industry event that took place today in Berlin — hosted in collaboration with </span><span style="font-weight: 400;">Adobe</span><span style="font-weight: 400;">, </span><span style="font-weight: 400;">Bertelsmann</span><span style="font-weight: 400;">, NVIDIA and </span><span style="font-weight: 400;">UFA</span><span style="font-weight: 400;"> — the latest advancements in intelligent media were on display.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Key to the news: the </span><span style="font-weight: 400;">European Broadcasting Union (EBU)</span><span style="font-weight: 400;"> is collaborating with NVIDIA to expand its sovereign framework with cloud, open models and integration partners, while </span><span style="font-weight: 400;">Adobe</span><span style="font-weight: 400;"> Frame.io is accelerating video agent search capabilities with NVIDIA GPUs, helping German TV and film producer </span><span style="font-weight: 400;">UFA</span><span style="font-weight: 400;"> find the right moments faster.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Read more about both announcements below.</span></p>
<h2><b>Sovereign Clouds and Open Models Built for European Broadcasters</b></h2>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">The </span><span style="font-weight: 400;">EBU</span><span style="font-weight: 400;"> and NVIDIA are expanding the sovereign standards they </span><a href="https://blogs.nvidia.com/blog/european-broadcasting-union-sovereign-ai/"><span style="font-weight: 400;">introduced</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;"> last year for European public service media. The initiative now brings in sovereign cloud providers, open model developers and systems integrators to define how broadcasters can deploy agents while keeping control of their data, infrastructure, models and security.</span></p>
<div style="margin: 0 !important; padding: 0 !important; text-align: center; line-height: 1;"><a style="display: inline-flex; align-items: center; justify-content: center; margin: 0 !important; padding: 10px !important; background-color: #76b900; color: #ffffff !important; font-family: 'NVIDIA Sans', Arial, sans-serif; font-size: 16px; font-weight: bold; line-height: 1 !important; text-align: center; text-decoration: none !important; white-space: nowrap; vertical-align: top;" href="https://blogs.nvidia.com/gtc-berlin-2026-news/">Read All NVIDIA in Europe Announcements</a></div>
<p>&nbsp;</p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">The </span><span style="font-weight: 400;">EBU</span><span style="font-weight: 400;"> is the world’s leading alliance of public service media, with over 115 member organizations in more than 55 countries reaching over 1 billion people. As agents move into newsrooms, production and audience services, those broadcasters need a foundation that protects editorial independence, data sovereignty and national resilience.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">The expanded initiative is turning those principles into deployable services. It’ll define requirements and reference architectures for where workloads run, how data is governed, which models power services and how systems stay secure throughout their lifecycle.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">“These standards give EBU members a shared foundation to strengthen resilience, retain control and deliver accountable advanced services that sustain audience trust,” said Annsofi Eriksson, chief technology officer at the EBU.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Cloud providers will map the standards to sovereign deployment options: </span></p>
<ul>
<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Microsoft</span><span style="font-weight: 400;"> will bring expertise from Microsoft Sovereign Cloud, including Azure Local and Foundry Local, so broadcasters can process sensitive editorial material locally and keep critical services running when external connectivity is down.</span></li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Nebius</span><span style="font-weight: 400;"> offers a platform for broadcasters to train, fine-tune and run their own models on European infrastructure.</span></li>
</ul>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Open models give broadcasters the visibility to inspect, evaluate and adapt AI for local languages, archives and editorial policies. </span><a target="_blank" href="https://www.nvidia.com/en-us/ai-data-science/foundation-models/nemotron/"><span style="font-weight: 400;">NVIDIA Nemotron</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;"> open models provide a foundation broadcasters can customize for domain-specific applications and public-service requirements.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">“Sovereign cloud infrastructure and open models can help Europe’s public service media innovate while protecting data, editorial independence and cultural diversity,” said Delphine Ernotte, CEO of France Télévisions and president of the </span><span style="font-weight: 400;">EBU</span><span style="font-weight: 400;">.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Media-specific consulting and systems integration provided from </span><span style="font-weight: 400;">Qvest</span><span style="font-weight: 400;"> and other system integrators to ensure that reference architectures are practical, interoperable and deployable in real world broadcast environments. </span></p>
<h2><b>Adobe</b><b> Frame.io Speeds Video Search With NVIDIA Accelerated Computing</b></h2>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Also at the event, </span><span style="font-weight: 400;">Adobe</span><span style="font-weight: 400;"> is highlighting its latest enhancements that help creatives search through video data faster.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Adobe </span><span style="font-weight: 400;">is using NVIDIA accelerated computing to help creative teams find and work with video content faster. Its GPU-accelerated search pipeline uses </span><a target="_blank" href="https://developer.nvidia.com/video-codec-sdk"><span style="font-weight: 400;">NVIDIA NVDEC </span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;">for video decoding, </span><a target="_blank" href="https://developer.nvidia.com/tensorrt"><span style="font-weight: 400;">TensorRT</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;"> for optimized AI inference and the </span><a target="_blank" href="https://developer.nvidia.com/cuda-zone"><span style="font-weight: 400;">CUDA platform</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;"> for preprocessing and embedding generation. </span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Adobe</span><span style="font-weight: 400;"> is also advancing GPU-accelerated media ingest using NVIDIA NVENC and NVDEC to accelerate video encoding and decoding, helping production teams process growing media libraries more efficiently.  </span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">The results: about 5x faster embedding generation, up to 4x higher throughput and up to 5x lower latency for search processing. A new two-frame-per-second proxy, about 80% smaller than the 360p version it replaces, cuts storage and transfer while keeping the visual detail search needs.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">That speed matters for </span><span style="font-weight: 400;">UFA</span><span style="font-weight: 400;">, one of Germany’s leading film and TV producers. It delivers more than 3,500 hours of programming a year, including the daily drama “Gute Zeiten, schlechte Zeiten.” When editors build a recap or flashback, they may need a specific reaction or unresolved exchange from as far back as 15 years — something file names and folders can’t surface.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Frame.io semantic search finds moments inside videos by concept, action or setting, and hybrid search adds notes, transcripts and approval status. An editor can search for “a character looking concerned,” narrow by character name and filter to approved footage, then move straight into review.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">The work is part of a broader </span><span style="font-weight: 400;">Adobe</span><span style="font-weight: 400;"> and NVIDIA collaboration that also spans </span><span style="font-weight: 400;">Adobe</span><span style="font-weight: 400;"> Substance 3D, Firefly Foundry and 3D digital twin workflows for enterprises.</span></p>

										<content:encoded><![CDATA[<div id="bsf_rt_marker"></div><p><span style="font-weight: 400;">NVIDIA today released Nemotron 3.5 Super VL, an open 120-billion-parameter model that delivers the fastest task completion and highest accuracy for specialized always-on agents.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Joining it are two new edge models: Nemotron 3.5 Bolt 74B-A9B 24GB, optimized to balance intelligence and speed per GB for local agentic workloads, and Nemotron 3.5 Bolt E4B 4GB, optimized for intelligence per GB for fast conversational agents.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Super VL and Bolt 74B-A9B are available now, with Bolt E4B coming Oct. 23.</span><b><i><br />
</i></b><b><br />
</b><span style="font-weight: 400;">The three </span><a target="_blank" href="https://www.nvidia.com/en-us/glossary/open-models/"><span style="font-weight: 400;">open models</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;"> together cover the full range of agent deployments, from large enterprise servers down to edge devices and local hardware. </span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Any organization that wants to run agents on NVIDIA infrastructure now has an open, optimized model for their tier, without having to choose between capability and deployability. </span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Super VL and Bolt join the Nemotron family alongside </span><a href="https://blogs.nvidia.com/blog/nemotron-lightning-switchyard-rtx-dgx/"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Lightning</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;"> and Ultra. </span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Lightning, released in August, handles high-throughput workloads; Ultra handles the hardest reasoning tasks; and Bolt brings intelligence to the edge. </span></p>
<h2><b>Nemotron 3.5 Super VL: Frontier Accuracy at One-Fifth the Size</b></h2>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Super VL, built in collaboration with the </span><a target="_blank" href="https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/nvidia-launches-nemotron-coalition-of-leading-global-ai-labs-to-advance-open-frontier-models"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Nemotron Coalition</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;">, delivers leading accuracy on multimodal agentic tasks — instruction following, tool use, multistep business workflows, and visual understanding — comparable with NVIDIA’s largest reasoning model at roughly one-fifth the parameter count.<br />
</span></p>

<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">As the fastest open agentic model in its class — 5x faster than the next best — it helps enterprise agents complete high-volume tasks faster across workflows that span days or weeks. </span></p>

<h2>Nemotron 3.5 Bolt: Agentic Intelligence at the Edge</h2>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Bolt 74B-A9B 24GB is designed to run everyday agentic tasks locally on NVIDIA RTX/DGX Spark, using a native 2-bit quantization to fit in 24GB of memory and run alongside other applications.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Relative to other models with a similar memory footprint, it provides a good balance of intelligence and speed: smart enough to get most jobs done, yet much faster than the smartest models.</span></p>

<h2>What Nemotron 3.5 Super VL Actually Sees</h2>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Nemotron 3.5 Super VL&#8217;s </span><a target="_blank" href="https://www.nvidia.com/en-us/glossary/vision-language-models/"><span style="font-weight: 400;">vision language</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;"> strengths are targeted at what agents encounter in practice: optical character recognition, tables, charts and structured information in visual documents. </span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">It processes a web page, extracts text, reads graphs and charts, and returns insights from what it finds. The same applies to PDFs and other documents agents commonly work with.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Nemotron 3.5 Super VL understands images, webpages and PDFs and acts on that understanding. Combining text and visual understanding in one model means agents don&#8217;t need to route tasks between separate systems — context carries through as work moves from document to data to decision. </span></p>
<h2><b>Open for Maximum Control, Security, and Privacy</b></h2>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Openness and customizability reinforce each other. The model ships with open weights, open datasets and training recipes.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Control runs in two directions. Organizations can run the model wherever their security and data-residency requirements dictate. They also choose to adopt a model they can adapt, rather than one subject to a provider&#8217;s restrictions.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">In insurance, for example, a customized agent can scan claims for fraud markers on repeat. In financial services, an agent can review credit scores and flag activity against an organization&#8217;s own lending criteria. The model stays on premises. The data stays private.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Customization is supported through NVIDIA NeMo, Python-based pipelines and open datasets.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Nemotron 3.5 Super VL was trained on about 26 trillion tokens across </span><a target="_blank" href="https://huggingface.co/collections/nvidia/nemotron-pre-training-datasets"><span style="font-weight: 400;">pretraining</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;"> and continued pretraining, then post-trained with roughly 3.8 million samples and reinforcement learning across dozens of environments. </span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">NVIDIA is also releasing open datasets on Hugging Face spanning </span><a target="_blank" href="https://huggingface.co/collections/nvidia/nemotron-post-training-v3"><span style="font-weight: 400;">agentic tool use</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;">, finance, instruction following, long context, fairness and vision-language understanding, along with the reinforcement learning environments in NeMo Gym.</span></p>
<p><a target="_blank" href="https://www.nvidia.com/en-us/ai-data-science/products/nim-microservices/"><span style="font-weight: 400;">NVIDIA NIM</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;"> packages the model as a production-ready microservice with performance-optimized, validated configurations and enterprise-grade support, refreshed every two weeks with security patches and performance improvements.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">And with the model running on NVIDIA Dynamo, organizations can serve up to 8x more users with 85% lower latency.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Nemotron 3.5 Super VL is available now on Hugging Face, Open Router, </span><a target="_blank" href="https://modelscope.ai/collections/nv-community/Nemotron-35-Super-VL"><span style="font-weight: 400;">ModelScope</span><span style="font-weight: 400;"> community</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;"> and as NVIDIA NIM.</span></p>

										<content:encoded><![CDATA[<div id="bsf_rt_marker"></div><p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Much of today’s scientific progress begins with computation. </span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">But turning accelerated computing’s potential into scientific progress — modeling climate systems, designing molecules, simulating materials and more — depends on more than breakthrough technology. Especially for smaller labs, early-career scientists or science-based startups, the hardest part of research can be everything that comes before the science: assembling tools, finding compute and building the expertise to use both. </span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">At the AI in Science Summit in Dublin today, the </span><span style="font-weight: 400;">European Commission</span><span style="font-weight: 400;"> launched the AI in Science Enablers Initiative, a shared framework connected to RAISE — the Resource for AI Science in Europe — that brings private-sector support closer to Europe’s research community. </span></p>
<div style="margin: 0 !important; padding: 0 !important; text-align: center; line-height: 1;"><a style="display: inline-flex; align-items: center; justify-content: center; margin: 0 !important; padding: 10px !important; background-color: #76b900; color: #ffffff !important; font-family: 'NVIDIA Sans', Arial, sans-serif; font-size: 16px; font-weight: bold; line-height: 1 !important; text-align: center; text-decoration: none !important; white-space: nowrap; vertical-align: top;" href="https://blogs.nvidia.com/gtc-berlin-2026-news/">Read All NVIDIA in Europe Announcements</a></div>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;"><br />
NVIDIA is </span><a target="_blank" href="https://research-and-innovation.ec.europa.eu/strategy/strategy-research-and-innovation/our-digital-future/european-ai-science-strategy_en"><span style="font-weight: 400;">joining the campaign</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;">, providing:</span></p>
<ul>
<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Access to accelerated tools, models and platforms</span></li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Compute, cloud resources, data and scientific resources</span></li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Skills, training and technical support</span></li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Collaborative projects and pilots along with support for researchers and talent</span></li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Support for startups and ecosystem development</span></li>
</ul>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">“Europe’s ambition in AI in science can only become reality if we bring together the strengths of our research community, industry and other partners,” Ekaterina Zaharieva, European Commissioner for Startups, Research and Innovation. “Through the AI in Science Enablers campaign, we are turning this ambition into concrete commitments that give researchers access to the tools, expertise and resources they need to innovate.”</span></p>
<h2><b>Open Models</b></h2>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">NVIDIA’s pledge begins with open source technology. </span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">NVIDIA has released more than 250 open models, 250 open datasets and over 1,400 open source software projects spanning large language models, healthcare, robotics, climate science, physical AI, autonomous vehicles and scientific research. The models and datasets are available on Hugging Face and GitHub.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Building on this open ecosystem, researchers can build agentic reasoning capabilities with </span><a target="_blank" href="https://www.nvidia.com/en-us/ai-data-science/foundation-models/nemotron/"><span style="font-weight: 400;">NVIDIA Nemotron</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;"> — a family of architectures, open weights, open source models, training data and recipes — and scientific software including </span><a target="_blank" href="https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/nvidia-launches-bionemo-agent-toolkit-giving-ai-agents-the-tools-to-accelerate-scientific-discovery"><span style="font-weight: 400;">NVIDIA BioNeMo Agent Toolkit</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;">, </span><a target="_blank" href="https://developer.nvidia.com/physicsnemo"><span style="font-weight: 400;">NVIDIA PhysicsNeMo</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;"> libraries, </span><a target="_blank" href="https://www.nvidia.com/en-us/high-performance-computing/earth-2/"><span style="font-weight: 400;">NVIDIA Earth-2</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;"> models and </span><a target="_blank" href="https://developer.nvidia.com/cuda/cuda-x-libraries/alchemi"><span style="font-weight: 400;">NVIDIA ALCHEMI NIM microservices</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;"> to accelerate research across biology, chemistry, molecular design, weather and climate, energy, engineering, physics and material sciences. </span></p>
<h2><b>Skills and Training </b></h2>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Building advanced intelligence capability across Europe requires knowledge that travels as freely as compute. </span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">The NVIDIA Deep Learning Institute has launched a new</span> <a target="_blank" href="https://www.nvidia.com/en-eu/training/open-source/"><span style="font-weight: 400;">open source courseware hub</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;"> with hands-on course materials covering accelerated computing and deep learning — freely downloadable and openly licensed, with no enrollment, fees or deadline. </span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Modular by design, the courses can be adapted for university curricula, supercomputing center bootcamps or developer community events, and serve as a direct bridge to NVIDIA’s broader</span><a target="_blank" href="https://www.nvidia.com/en-eu/training/"> <span style="font-weight: 400;">training and certification programs</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;">.</span></p>
<h2><b>Access to Compute</b></h2>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">NVIDIA is collaborating with cloud infrastructure partners and European national AI factories — including in </span><a href="https://blogs.nvidia.com/blog/france-ecosystem-agentic-computing"><span style="font-weight: 400;">France</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;">, Spain and </span><a href="https://blogs.nvidia.com/blog/nordics-technology-ecosystem"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Sweden</span></a> <span style="font-weight: 400;">— to open new routes for startups and small- and medium-sized enterprises (SMEs) to access sovereign European compute and services. </span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">The </span><a target="_blank" href="https://www.nvidia.com/en-eu/startups/"><span style="font-weight: 400;">NVIDIA Inception</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;"> program for startups provides access to compute resources and technical support, lowering the barrier to entry for startups seeking high-performance infrastructure and technological education. </span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">AI2F</span><span style="font-weight: 400;"> in France is the first AI factory to have collaborated with the Inception program for four months, demonstrating strong results.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">The </span><span style="font-weight: 400;">BSC</span><span style="font-weight: 400;"> AI Factory ran a pilot initiative and is now rolling out its first full accelerator program, with growing interest from other European national AI factories in replicating the model in 2027. Beyond startup support, this program is designed to contribute to a broader strategic objective: building self-sustaining AI ventures in Spain and Europe.</span></p>
<p><a target="_blank" href="https://www.ri.se/en/news/sweden-ai-factory-and-nvidia-collaborate-to-shape-the-future-of-swedish-ai"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Sweden AI Factory</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;">, one of Europe’s 19 EuroHPC-funded AI factories, announced a collaboration with NVIDIA to open its infrastructure and expertise to Inception startups and SMEs enterprises across sectors including life and material sciences, autonomous systems and gaming. </span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Finally, EuroHPC supercomputing centers such as </span><span style="font-weight: 400;">CINECA</span><span style="font-weight: 400;"> and </span><span style="font-weight: 400;">JSC </span><span style="font-weight: 400;">provide academia and startups with free access to NVIDIA GPU infrastructure and services for open research. NVIDIA complements this access with office hours, bootcamps and hackathons that help researchers fine-tune models on their own datasets.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">As an example, NVIDIA is co-organizing an European AI hackathon this month alongside </span><span style="font-weight: 400;">Austrian Scientific Computing</span><span style="font-weight: 400;">, the</span><span style="font-weight: 400;"> Leibniz Supercomputing Centre</span><span style="font-weight: 400;">, the </span><span style="font-weight: 400;">High-Performance Computing Center Stuttgart</span><span style="font-weight: 400;"> and </span><span style="font-weight: 400;">The Cyprus Institute, for Pharos-CY</span><span style="font-weight: 400;">.</span></p>
<h2><b>AI Infrastructure Expansion</b></h2>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">To top it all off, NVIDIA is powering Europe’s most ambitious expansion of AI infrastructure to date, with </span><a target="_blank" href="https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/europe-unveils-a-record-35-new-nvidia-ai-supercomputers"><span style="font-weight: 400;">35 supercomputers</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;"> currently in development across the continent — the largest single-year buildout of supercomputing capacity Europe has ever seen.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Together, these systems will equip </span><a target="_blank" href="https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/europe-unveils-a-record-35-new-nvidia-ai-supercomputers"><span style="font-weight: 400;">over 3 million researchers</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;"> with next-generation infrastructure, accelerating discovery across domains from climate science and drug development to materials research and beyond.</span></p>
<p><i><span style="font-weight: 400;">Explore </span></i><a target="_blank" href="https://www.nvidia.com/en-us/high-performance-computing/hpc-and-ai/"><i><span style="font-weight: 400;">NVIDIA technologies for science</span></i></a><i><span style="font-weight: 400;"> and </span></i><a target="_blank" href="https://www.nvidia.com/en-eu/training/open-source/"><i><span style="font-weight: 400;">open source courseware</span></i></a><i><span style="font-weight: 400;">, and find hands-on training through the </span></i><a target="_blank" href="https://www.nvidia.com/en-eu/training/"><i><span style="font-weight: 400;">NVIDIA Deep Learning Institute</span></i></a><i><span style="font-weight: 400;">. </span></i></p>

										<content:encoded><![CDATA[<div id="bsf_rt_marker"></div><p><span style="font-weight: 400">The Nordic countries have become the European leader in unicorn startup density, producing the most emerging companies valued at $1 billion or more per capita on the continent. </span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400">Now, a new chapter for the region is unfolding as countries race to build one of Europe’s most consequential technology ecosystems.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400">The many computing advancements already underway in the Nordics, combined with the opportunity that lies ahead, signal a region moving from ambition to application.</span></p>
<h2><b>NVIDIA’s Investments in the Nordics</b></h2>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400">Over the past year, NVIDIA has invested in the technology ecosystem in the Nordics, including Sweden’s leading startups</span> <span style="font-weight: 400">Lovable</span> <span style="font-weight: 400">and </span><span style="font-weight: 400">Legora.</span></p>
<p>&nbsp;</p>
<div style="margin: 0 !important;padding: 0 !important;text-align: center;line-height: 1"><a style="display: inline-flex;align-items: center;justify-content: center;margin: 0 !important;padding: 10px !important;background-color: #76b900;color: #ffffff !important;font-family: &#039;NVIDIA Sans&#039;, Arial, sans-serif;font-size: 16px;font-weight: bold;line-height: 1 !important;text-align: center;text-decoration: none !important;white-space: nowrap;vertical-align: top" href="https://blogs.nvidia.com/gtc-berlin-2026-news/">Read All NVIDIA in Europe Announcements</a></div>
<p>&nbsp;</p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400">Lovable</span><span style="font-weight: 400">, a Stockholm-based startup, provides a software creation platform that enables anyone to build and run software using plain language. In collaboration with NVIDIA, Lovable is optimizing inference for open models and training its models using NVIDIA Dynamo software and Nemotron tools.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400">Legora</span><span style="font-weight: 400"> provides an agentic operating system for legal work, supporting lawyers in research, review and drafting across complex matters.</span><span style="font-weight: 400"> </span></p>
<h2><b>Nordics Emerge as Infrastructure Hub</b></h2>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400">The foundation for establishing the Nordics as a technology powerhouse is already being built and the region holds a structural advantage: abundant renewable energy, rigorous data governance and a research tradition that bridges academia and industry.  </span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400">The Nordics are one of Europe’s fastest growing hubs for infrastructure. AI clouds are expanding with a projected 1 gigawatts of capacity to be deployed across the region by the end of 2027. Among these cloud providers are </span><span style="font-weight: 400">Bitdeer,</span> <span style="font-weight: 400">CoreWeave</span><span style="font-weight: 400">, </span><span style="font-weight: 400">Crusoe</span><span style="font-weight: 400">, </span><span style="font-weight: 400">Hyperstack,</span> <span style="font-weight: 400">Nscale</span><span style="font-weight: 400">, </span><span style="font-weight: 400">Verda</span><span style="font-weight: 400"> and </span><span style="font-weight: 400">Volta</span><span style="font-weight: 400">. </span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400">In Sweden, a consortium anchored by </span><span style="font-weight: 400">Ericsson, AstraZeneca, Saab, SEB and Wallenberg Investments, </span><span style="font-weight: 400">with </span><span style="font-weight: 400">SKF </span><span style="font-weight: 400">as strategic partner, launched </span><a target="_blank" href="https://sferical.ai/newsroom/leading-swedish-companies-launch-sferical-one-shared-ai-supercomputer"><span style="font-weight: 400">Sferical One</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400"> — a shared supercomputer running on two </span><a target="_blank" href="https://www.nvidia.com/en-us/data-center/dgx-superpod/"><span style="font-weight: 400">NVIDIA DGX SuperPODs</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400"> with 16 racks of </span><a target="_blank" href="https://www.nvidia.com/en-us/data-center/dgx-gb300/"><span style="font-weight: 400">NVIDIA DGX GB300 systems</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400"> and 1,152 GPUs. The consortium will build services using </span><a target="_blank" href="https://www.nvidia.com/en-us/ai-data-science/foundation-models/nemotron/"><span style="font-weight: 400">NVIDIA Nemotron</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400"> open models, which allow researchers to customize and deploy their models in a secure environment.</span></p>
<h2><b>Nordic-Built Intelligence</b></h2>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400">Some of the Nordics’ leading technology companies are now building proprietary intelligence on NVIDIA infrastructure.</span></p>
<p><a target="_blank" href="https://engineering.atspotify.com/2026/10/advancing-generative-recommendation-inference-our-collaboration-with-nvidia-on-tensorrt-llm"><span style="font-weight: 400">Spotify</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400">, which personalizes music, podcasts and audiobooks for hundreds of millions of listeners, uses NVIDIA TensorRT-LLM inference software to run AI-powered podcast recommendations faster and cheaper at scale. The deployment is a case study in inference efficiency: delivering the same recommendations to a global audience at a lower cost per query.</span></p>
<p><a target="_blank" href="https://www.abb.com/global/en/news/137409/abb-robotics-and-nvidia-white-paper-defines-transformative-impact-of-physical-ai-on-manufacturing"><span style="font-weight: 400">ABB Robotics</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400"> developed RobotStudio HyperReality using </span><a target="_blank" href="https://www.nvidia.com/en-us/omniverse/"><span style="font-weight: 400">NVIDIA Omniverse</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400"> libraries to bridge the gap between robot simulations and real-world performance. By combining NVIDIA’s advanced simulation capabilities with ABB Robotics’ virtual controller, running the same firmware as its physical robots, the solution enables engineers to train, test and validate robots virtually, while using real-world data to continuously improve the digital model. According to ABB Robotics, the solution delivers up to 99% simulation accuracy and reduces commissioning time by up to 80%.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400">Finnish telecom company </span><a target="_blank" href="https://nam11.safelinks.protection.outlook.com/?url=https%3A%2F%2Fyrityksille.elisa.fi%2Fideat%2Fmoontalk-airi-real-time-ai-assistance&amp;data=05%7C02%7Cebeisswenger%40nvidia.com%7Ce4430ec4c92b4ed0c2d508df29bf8858%7C43083d15727340c1b7db39efd9ccc17a%7C0%7C0%7C639275574771855820%7CUnknown%7CTWFpbGZsb3d8eyJFbXB0eU1hcGkiOnRydWUsIlYiOiIwLjAuMDAwMCIsIlAiOiJXaW4zMiIsIkFOIjoiTWFpbCIsIldUIjoyfQ%3D%3D%7C0%7C%7C%7C&amp;sdata=%2Bv8nQ8VMmNnfXQBVAQfHXnCS8ch%2B68zVKKfA8xo6wq0%3D&amp;reserved=0"><span style="font-weight: 400">Elisa</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400"> is launching a beta of Moontalk AIRI, a real-time enterprise agentic assistant that retrieves information, captures insights and documents decisions during meetings and calls. Built on NVIDIA Nemotron open models and distributed infrastructure, it uses company data and low-latency networking to deliver fast, context-aware responses. </span></p>
<h2><b>Nordic Startups Scaling on NVIDIA</b></h2>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400">Sweden’s startup ecosystem raised $2.8 billion in the first half of 2026 and is projected to reach $5 billion for the year. This next generation is building fast — and on open models.</span></p>
<p><iframe loading="lazy" title="120ft Factory | Nordics and NVIDIA Show What’s Possible When a Region Commits to Advanced Computing" width="1200" height="675" src="https://www.youtube.com/embed/C4WMWTrUA6E?feature=oembed" frameborder="0" allow="accelerometer; autoplay; clipboard-write; encrypted-media; gyroscope; picture-in-picture; web-share" referrerpolicy="strict-origin-when-cross-origin" allowfullscreen></iframe></p>

The Stockholm-based startup is post-training NVIDIA Nemotron open models to scale patent analysis from a single patent to entire portfolios — work that was previously out of reach. Nemotron gives </span><span style="font-weight: 400">Stilta</span><span style="font-weight: 400"> the language model accuracy for continuous portfolio monitoring at scale, across the thousands of patents that large industrial and technology companies hold.</span></p>
<p><a target="_blank" href="https://confidentialmind.com/blog/sampotron-adapting-queries-reusing-index"><span style="font-weight: 400">ConfidentialMind’s SampoTron</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400">,</span><span style="font-weight: 400"> built on NVIDIA Nemotron-3-Embed, delivers more than 20% improvement in cross-lingual retrieval accuracy across English, Finnish and Swedish.</span></p>
<p><a target="_blank" href="https://www.agaton.ai/post/nvidia-nemotron"><span style="font-weight: 400">Agaton</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400">, a platform that analyzes customer conversations in real time, is using NVIDIA Nemotron 3.5 Lightning to speed up Revenue Mining solution on live sales calls. <p><a target="_blank" href="https://research.klang.ai/releases/pianissimo/"><span style="font-weight: 400">Klang</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400">, a Swedish speech lab, fine-tuned the NVIDIA Parakeet v3 model to create Pianissimo — a Swedish transcription model that reduces errors by 75% while being 64x faster than the most accurate alternative. As a result, local LLM downloads are already 5x higher than all of last year.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">From models to agent harnesses to security to tools, NVIDIA is partnering closely with the local AI ecosystem to make RTX Spark the ideal hardware to run local agents. </span></p>
<h4><b>Local AI Agents Made Easy On RTX Spark</b></h4>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">From the first boot of RTX Spark, users will be greeted with a “Getting Started” screen to choose and install their agent app.</span></p>
<p><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="alignnone wp-image-98815" src="https://blogs.nvidia.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/rtx-spark-partner-ecosystem-scaled.png" alt="" width="2048" height="1302" srcset="https://blogs.nvidia.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/rtx-spark-partner-ecosystem-scaled.png 2048w, https://blogs.nvidia.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/rtx-spark-partner-ecosystem-960x610.png 960w, https://blogs.nvidia.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/rtx-spark-partner-ecosystem-1680x1068.png 1680w, https://blogs.nvidia.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/rtx-spark-partner-ecosystem-1280x814.png 1280w, https://blogs.nvidia.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/rtx-spark-partner-ecosystem-1536x976.png 1536w, https://blogs.nvidia.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/rtx-spark-partner-ecosystem-630x400.png 630w" sizes="auto, (max-width: 2048px) 100vw, 2048px" /></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">These agents will come with new, secure sandboxing technology thanks to NVIDIA OpenShell and MXC. MXC delivers operating system-level security, while OpenShell makes it simpler for agents to adopt MXC and adds advanced capabilities such as credential protection and security monitoring. Hermes Agent will integrate granular OpenShell controls and MXC by the end of the month. OpenClaw and GitHub Copilot now support MXC.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">After installing an agent, setting up local AI is also now seamless in GitHub Copilot, Hermes Agent, OpenClaw, Perplexity, Pi and WorkBuddy. The apps will recommend an optimized local model and settings configuration for RTX Spark, and set it all up for users in a few clicks.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Many users want the best of both worlds — local models for free iteration, and cloud models for frontier intelligence. NVIDIA Switchyard, an open source model router library, enables agents to intelligently route queries between local and cloud LLMs, allowing the user to minimize cloud usage but still obtain similar results to just running on cloud. Hermes will be adding Switchyard later in October, and other agents like Perplexity Computer and GitHub Copilot have integrated their own routing solutions.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Software providers have made it simple for agents to connect to top Windows apps, with easy-to-use MCP libraries and command line interfaces (CLIs). NVIDIA has also now made available MCPs for its applications, including NVIDIA App, Broadcast, PAIR, Omniverse, RTX Remix and a large library of </span><a target="_blank" href="https://github.com/NVIDIA/skills"><span style="font-weight: 400;">CUDA-X Skills</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;">. </span></p>

<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">NVIDIA is also releasing </span><a target="_blank" href="http://build.nvidia.com/rtx-spark"><span style="font-weight: 400;">AI playbooks</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;"> for RTX Spark, available on Friday, Oct 16. Users can set up their first agent, build an AI chief of staff, configure their system for development or design a creative assistant.</span></p>
<h4><b>Meet Nemotron 3.5 Bolt 74B: Built for Local Agents on RTX Spark</b></h4>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">NVIDIA Nemotron 3.5 Bolt 74B-A9B 24GB, available Thursday, Oct. 15, is a new LLM purpose-built for running local agents on RTX Spark. </span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">This model is a 74B parameter, 9B-active-parameter mixture-of-experts (MoE) model, packed into 24GB using 2-bit quantization with advanced quantization-aware training techniques to retain quality. It aims to achieve the optimal balance between speed and intelligence, being faster than most popular dense models while smarter than most popular MoE models with a similar memory footprint.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Users will be able to run Nemotron 3.5 Bolt 74B at launch through llama.cpp, vLLM, Ollama, Unsloth and LM Studio. It will also be directly accessible via agents such as Hermes and OpenClaw.  </span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Like other Nemotron models, Bolt 74B is fully open source — including training data, weights and recipes — so developers can fine-tune and customize their own agents.</span></p>
<h4><b>The Perfect PAIR</b></h4>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Local AI doesn’t have to run on just one PC. </span><a target="_blank" href="https://www.nvidia.com/en-us/ai-on-rtx/personal-ai-router/"><span style="font-weight: 400;">NVIDIA PAIR</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;">, a personal AI router, is officially moving out of beta today, making it easier to tap into the total AI compute of compatible PCs on a local network. The free, open source software tool automatically discovers and connects devices, installs AI models and inference engines, and distributes AI workloads across available systems.</span></p>
<p><iframe loading="lazy" title="NVIDIA PAIR: Hermes 5-Subagent Demo" width="1200" height="675" src="https://www.youtube.com/embed/GjGM-ZKQMa0?feature=oembed" frameborder="0" allow="accelerometer; autoplay; clipboard-write; encrypted-media; gyroscope; picture-in-picture; web-share" referrerpolicy="strict-origin-when-cross-origin" allowfullscreen></iframe></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">The official release adds llama.cpp support, direct model downloads from Hugging Face, customizable inference engine settings and RTX Spark support, along with a variety of inference and user experience improvements. </span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">PAIR works on Windows, Linux and macOS. Developers can contribute to the open source project via </span><a target="_blank" href="https://github.com/NVIDIA/Personal-AI-Router"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Github</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;"> — just one month after the initial PAIR release, the community has already contributed over 10 features and fixes to the release. </span></p>
<h2><b>Sparking Creativity</b></h2>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">RTX Spark brings over 130 accelerated apps, CUDA for fast video and image generation, and RTX technologies such as DLSS, NVENC and OptiX to advanced content creation workflows — all in a thin and light laptop with on-the-go performance.</span></p>
<h4><b>Adobe Accelerates Creative Applications for RTX Spark</b></h4>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Adobe is optimizing its industry-leading creative applications, including Photoshop, Premiere and Substance 3D, for RTX Spark, delivering up to 2x faster AI, editing, color and effects across creative workflows. </span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">In Photoshop, GPU-accelerated compositing makes complex image editing faster and more responsive, with improvements to the Remove Tool and effects such as Light Leaks. Premiere accelerates demanding video editing tasks, including playback, rendering, color grading and AI-powered features such as Enhance Speech and Object Mask. Meanwhile, Substance 3D Painter speeds up texture creation workflows, from baking high-resolution mesh maps to opening, saving and exporting large projects.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">The updates will be rolling out in phases, with AI feature optimizations in Photoshop and Premiere now ready in beta, and with Adobe Substance 3D updates coming soon. Opt in to the beta to get access to the RTX Spark-optimized builds. </span></p>
<h4><b>Latest NVIDIA Broadcast Brings New AI Effects to Live and Recorded Content</b></h4>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Available for download now, </span><a target="_blank" href="https://www.nvidia.com/en-us/geforce/broadcasting/broadcast-app/"><span style="font-weight: 400;">NVIDIA Broadcast 2.3</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;"> runs natively on RTX Spark, and adds new RTX Video Super Resolution and Frame Generation AI effects and Studio Voice profiles.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">A new Offline File Processor lets creators apply Broadcast effects to prerecorded content, with many effects running at higher quality since there isn’t a need to maintain real-time performance. The update also introduces a new MCP server and skill that lets local AI agents adjust effects, change settings and process files directly through the Broadcast app.</span></p>
<h4><b>RTX Video Brings Frame Generation and HDR to ComfyUI</b></h4>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">ComfyUI is expanding its RTX Video tools with two new nodes: RTX Video Frame Generation and RTX Video HDR. It’s also providing the ability to export EXR files, giving creators more ways to enhance AI-generated video.</span></p>

<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">RTX Video Frame Generation increases video frame rates for smoother motion for GeForce RTX 40 Series GPUs and newer, while RTX Video HDR converts standard dynamic range (SDR) footage to HDR for greater flexibility in color grading. Combined with RTX Video Super Resolution, these tools enable creators to upscale, enhance and refine video within a single ComfyUI workflow.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">For example, creators can combine LTX 2.5 with RTX Video Frame Generation and HDR to transform a 1080p, 30 frames-per-second (fps) SDR clip into a smoother 60 fps HDR video exported as EXR files ready for color grading. </span></p>
<h4><b>Blender Adds DLSS 4.5 Ray Reconstruction</b></h4>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Blender has released a beta version of its popular 3D editor, adding compatibility for RTX Spark. Download </span><a target="_blank" href="https://builder.blender.org/download/daily/"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Blender version 5.3</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;"> to get access.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Coming next month, Blender will also be adding support for DLSS Ray Reconstruction, allowing for real-time updates in the viewport so artists can navigate a scene while previewing it in real time. </span></p>
<h2><b>Code, Test and Deploy From RTX Spark</b></h2>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Developers are moving from writing code to directing AI agents. This new development paradigm is requiring many to spread their work across their laptop, a cloud LLM and a cloud virtual machine, where they test and prototype. </span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">RTX Spark enables developers to work on one machine, running all their workloads and libraries, testing and prototyping, and deploying. They can now do so directly in Windows with the new Windows Developer Config, which enhances the user experience for devs and installs all the top developer tools — or via Windows Subsystem for Linux, where they can get access to the same packages they’ll use to publish to the cloud.</span></p>
<h4><b>Developer Tools Are RTX-Spark Ready</b></h4>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">NVIDIA has partnered with Microsoft to ensure that developer tools are ready for RTX Spark. Alongside RTX Spark, the community is releasing:</span></p>
<ul>
<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><span style="font-weight: 400;">The first PyTorch-CUDA build for Windows on Arm.</span></li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><span style="font-weight: 400;">The first Python build that is native by default on Windows on Arm, with Python 3.15.</span></li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><span style="font-weight: 400;">The first native Conda installer for Windows on Arm, with over 2,000 Conda packages.</span></li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><span style="font-weight: 400;">97% of the top 15,000 Python wheels are ready, thanks to work with the ecosystem. NVIDIA will be working closely with the community to ready the remaining wheels.</span></li>
</ul>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Development is now mostly done by agents. </span><a target="_blank" href="https://github.com/NVIDIA/skills"><span style="font-weight: 400;">NVIDIA Skills</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;"> — an open source GitHub repository that teaches agents how to use NVIDIA CUDA-X tools and libraries — lets users develop apps that are accelerated on NVIDIA technologies, even without knowing anything about CUDA.</span></p>
<h4><b>Coding Agents Are Ready for Local AI</b></h4>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">In addition to </span><a target="_blank" href="https://commandline.microsoft.com/local-models-sandboxed-tools-github-windows/"><span style="font-weight: 400;">GitHub Copilot</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;"> — one of the most popular coding agents, used by millions worldwide — adding support for MXC, it’s updating its model router </span><a target="_blank" href="https://github.blog/ai-and-ml/github-copilot/project-hydrafusion-frontier-quality-via-multi-model-orchestration/?utm_source=blog-cta-project-hydrafusion-article"><span style="font-weight: 400;">HydraFusion</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;"> with support for local models. This enables developers to use their local hardware to reduce token consumption while tapping into frontier intelligence.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Microsoft is making coding models, such as MAI 1.1 Coding Flash, </span><a target="_blank" href="https://blogs.windows.com/windowsexperience/2026/10/07/building-windows-for-hybrid-intelligence/#:~:text=Frontier%20coding%20intelligence%2C%20running%20locally%20on%20your%20PC"><span style="font-weight: 400;">available locally</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;">, and adding llama.cpp as a backend to Windows ML — extending the supported models and enabling the latest NVIDIA performance optimizations.</span></p>
<p><a target="_blank" href="https://docs.github.com/en/copilot/concepts/copilot-surfaces?utm_source=blog-linking-surfaces-documentation&amp;utm_medium=blog&amp;utm_campaign=oct-7-event-oct-2026"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Get started</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;"> with GitHub Copilot running locally when RTX Spark launches.</span></p>
<h4><b>Llama.cpp Adds Support for Decision Models, Releases a Windows App</b></h4>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Decision models are the latest breakthrough in AI development. They give AI agents a near-instant way to make decisions, without lengthy reasoning loops. They can choose what to do next, from classifying requests to selecting the next action, up to 200x faster than traditional agents. Decision models are now supported in llama.cpp and Unsloth on RTX Spark and GeForce RTX GPUs.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Llama.cpp is also releasing today </span><a target="_blank" href="https://github.com/ggml-org/Llama-Windows"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Llama Windows</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;">, a Windows-native companion app for llama.cpp, enabling an easy-to-use user interface to download and run models locally, without having to go into the command line. This app, like llama.cpp, is optimized for RTX Spark. Learn more on the </span><a target="_blank" href="https://huggingface.co/blog/huggingface/llama-windows"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Hugging Face blog</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;">.</span></p>

										<content:encoded><![CDATA[<div id="bsf_rt_marker"></div><p><span style="font-weight: 400;">As AI factories grow from megawatts to gigawatts, building them takes more than advanced computing. Open standards are helping the industry scale AI infrastructure faster, more efficiently and more reliably.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">At the Open Compute Project (OCP) Global Summit, taking place in the San Jose McEnery Convention Center, NVIDIA announced new partnerships and contributions aimed at creating a common foundation for next-generation AI infrastructure. </span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">NVIDIA contributed key </span><a target="_blank" href="https://www.nvidia.com/en-us/data-center/technologies/rubin/"><span style="font-weight: 400;">NVIDIA Vera Rubin</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;"> rack and compute tray designs to OCP through the open </span><a target="_blank" href="https://www.nvidia.com/en-us/data-center/products/mgx/"><span style="font-weight: 400;">NVIDIA MGX</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;"> architecture, giving system builders a common foundation for developing next-generation AI factory infrastructure.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Together, these efforts span the AI factory itself, the utility grid that powers it and the rack-scale architecture at the heart of modern accelerated computing.</span></p>
<h2><b>NVIDIA and Partners Announce Open Architecture Advances </b></h2>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">At the OCP event, NVIDIA and partners made a handful of announcements: </span></p>
<ul>
<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><span style="font-weight: 400;">NVIDIA and hyperscalers published open standards for next-generation AI factories, including AI factory design standards and grid resilience.</span><b> </b></li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><span style="font-weight: 400;">NVIDIA launched new open source software components for NVIDIA DSX AI Factory. </span></li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Meta </span><span style="font-weight: 400;">and NVIDIA advanced open AI networking with NVIDIA Spectrum-X Ethernet SuperNIC and Spectrum-6 innovations.</span></li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Visionbay</span> <span style="font-weight: 400;">introduced an 800V DC AI factory design for the Vera Rubin era.</span></li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><span style="font-weight: 400;">NVIDIA and </span><span style="font-weight: 400;">Cisco </span><span style="font-weight: 400;">announced the Ultra-Dense Small Form Factor Pluggable (USFP) standard, the industry’s first module ​for 400Gb/s-based networking fabrics.</span></li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><span style="font-weight: 400;">NVIDIA and </span><span style="font-weight: 400;">Google </span><span style="font-weight: 400;">announced Falcon Transport support on future NVIDIA ConnectX-10 SuperNICs and BlueField DPUs.</span></li>
</ul>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Before a packed audience, Ian Buck, vice president of hyperscale and high-performance computing at NVIDIA, discussed how open infrastructure is becoming a critical ingredient for scaling AI factories worldwide. </span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">The </span><a target="_blank" href="https://www.nvidia.com/en-us/events/ocp-summit/"><span style="font-weight: 400;">news at OCP</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;"> spotlights how NVIDIA’s extreme codesign combined with open standards is accelerating deployment, improving power efficiency and driving tokens-per-watt performance.</span></p>

<h2><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="alignnone wp-image-98712 size-full" src="https://blogs.nvidia.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/end-to-end-render-800-vdc-8k-5656065-scaled.png" alt="" width="2048" height="1152" srcset="https://blogs.nvidia.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/end-to-end-render-800-vdc-8k-5656065-scaled.png 2048w, https://blogs.nvidia.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/end-to-end-render-800-vdc-8k-5656065-960x540.png 960w, https://blogs.nvidia.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/end-to-end-render-800-vdc-8k-5656065-1680x945.png 1680w, https://blogs.nvidia.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/end-to-end-render-800-vdc-8k-5656065-1280x720.png 1280w, https://blogs.nvidia.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/end-to-end-render-800-vdc-8k-5656065-1536x864.png 1536w, https://blogs.nvidia.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/end-to-end-render-800-vdc-8k-5656065-1290x725.png 1290w, https://blogs.nvidia.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/end-to-end-render-800-vdc-8k-5656065-630x354.png 630w, https://blogs.nvidia.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/end-to-end-render-800-vdc-8k-5656065-300x169.png 300w, https://blogs.nvidia.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/end-to-end-render-800-vdc-8k-5656065-400x225.png 400w" sizes="auto, (max-width: 2048px) 100vw, 2048px" /></h2>
<h2 id="mgx" class="wp-block-heading" style="font-size: 24px;"><b>Vera Rubin AI Factories Advance With MGX Rack Contributions at OCP </b><b></b><a href="https://blogs.nvidia.com/blog/ocp-global-summit-vera-rubin-scale-ai-factories/#mgx"><b><img src="https://s.w.org/images/core/emoji/17.0.2/72x72/1f517.png" alt="🔗" class="wp-smiley" style="height: 1em; max-height: 1em;" /></b></a></h2>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">NVIDIA joined industry leaders in contributing to the third-generation MGX AI data center reference design through OCP’s Open DC Initiative.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Combined with open liquid-cooling technologies such as MQD and MQDB, the effort helps turn proven AI factory designs into industry standards that others can adopt, manufacture and scale.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">For NVIDIA Vera Rubin AI factories, the updated MGX 1.2 specification extends the open architecture for AI factories with rack-scale blind-mate liquid cooling, interoperable rack-scale interconnect infrastructure and a liquid-cooled 5,000A busbar designed for higher-power AI systems. It also adds native 19-inch equipment support and higher-density rack designs.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">MGX 1.2 further expands modularity with configurable I/O bays, fanless liquid-cooled compute trays, standardized power and cooling interfaces, and a common infrastructure that helps ecosystem partners build interoperable accelerated computing platforms. It improves assembly, test and service times to streamline manufacturing and field service to enable faster and more efficient infrastructure buildout. The architecture supports rack deployments up to 250kW while simplifying serviceability, deployment and infrastructure reuse.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">The Vera CPU rack design specification extends the NVIDIA MGX open architecture beyond accelerated computing, providing a purpose-built platform for the growing agentic AI era. While Vera Rubin NVL72 serves as the foundation for large-scale training and inference, the </span><a target="_blank" href="https://www.nvidia.com/en-us/data-center/vera-cpu/"><span style="font-weight: 400;">NVIDIA Vera CPU</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;"> rack is designed to support tool calling, orchestration and sandboxed execution, helping AI agents interact with applications, data and services in real time.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Built on the third-generation NVIDIA MGX rack architecture, the Vera CPU rack shares the same rack-scale design principles that unite compute, networking, power and cooling into a common modular platform. This approach enables partners to deploy specialized infrastructure while benefiting from a shared ecosystem, simplified integration and a consistent foundation for next-generation AI factories.</span></p>

<h2 id="open-standards" class="wp-block-heading" style="font-size: 24px;"><b>NVIDIA and Hyperscalers Publish Open Standards for Next-Generation AI Factories </b><b></b><a href="https://blogs.nvidia.com/blog/ocp-global-summit-vera-rubin-scale-ai-factories/#open-standards"><b><img src="https://s.w.org/images/core/emoji/17.0.2/72x72/1f517.png" alt="🔗" class="wp-smiley" style="height: 1em; max-height: 1em;" /></b></a></h2>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">For AI infrastructure to scale globally, it needs more than advanced hardware — it needs a common foundation that the entire industry can build on.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">At OCP, NVIDIA joined </span><span style="font-weight: 400;">AMD, Google </span><span style="font-weight: 400;">and </span><span style="font-weight: 400;">Meta t</span><span style="font-weight: 400;">o publish a new open specification that establishes that foundation. The </span><a target="_blank" href="https://www.opencompute.org/documents/open-data-center-spec-revision-0-7-0-4-pdf"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Open Data Center Specification v0.7</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;"> defines a unified blueprint for next-generation AI factory facility design — from power architecture to cooling infrastructure to physical layout. </span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">The </span><a target="_blank" href="https://www.opencompute.org/documents/ocp-base-spec-interface-specification-between-hyperscalers-and-utility-grid-for-disturbance-performance-pdf"><span style="font-weight: 400;">OCP Grid Resilience Specification</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;">, published by </span><span style="font-weight: 400;">Google</span><span style="font-weight: 400;">, Meta, Microsoft and NVIDIA, establishes how large-scale AI infrastructure should behave during utility grid disturbances: staying connected, responding automatically and restoring to near-full load within less than two seconds.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">With these standards now open, manufacturers around the world have a common target to build for. Equipment makers, colocation providers and data center operators can begin designing, fabricating and delivering the infrastructure layer that powers AI factories under construction globally — without waiting for proprietary specifications from individual customers.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Being </span><a target="_blank" href="https://docs.nvidia.com/dsx"><span style="font-weight: 400;">NVIDIA DSX reference design</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;">-compatible, these specifications provide a validated, deployable implementation of both specs, from facility layout and power architecture to the software that monitors and responds to grid signals in real time. For operators building Vera Rubin AI factories, there’s now a straight line from open community standard to a running system.</span></p>

<h2 id="visionbay" class="wp-block-heading" style="font-size: 24px;"><b>Visionbay.ai Designs 800VDC AI Factory for the Vera Rubin Era </b><b></b><a href="https://blogs.nvidia.com/blog/ocp-global-summit-vera-rubin-scale-ai-factories/#visionbay"><b><img src="https://s.w.org/images/core/emoji/17.0.2/72x72/1f517.png" alt="🔗" class="wp-smiley" style="height: 1em; max-height: 1em;" /></b></a></h2>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">As AI factories grow larger and more powerful, one challenge looms over them all: delivering enough power to the accelerated computing systems driving the next generation of AI.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">At OCP, </span><span style="font-weight: 400;">Visionbay </span><span style="font-weight: 400;">unveiled plans for K-1, a next-generation AI factory platform designed around an </span><a href="https://blogs.nvidia.com/blog/800-vdc-power-architecture-ai-factory"><span style="font-weight: 400;">800 VDC architecture</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;"> and built with future NVIDIA Vera Rubin deployments in mind. The project combines NVIDIA’s 800 VDC reference architecture with power and thermal technologies from </span><span style="font-weight: 400;">Vertiv </span><span style="font-weight: 400;">to create a scalable foundation for high-density AI infrastructure.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">The design addresses a growing industry challenge. As AI training, inference and reasoning workloads expand, rack power densities continue to rise. Traditional low-voltage power distribution becomes increasingly difficult to scale, requiring more copper, more cabling and more power-conversion equipment.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">By adopting NVIDIA’s 800 VDC architecture, K-1 creates a path toward higher-density AI infrastructure while preserving flexibility for existing deployments. Rather than requiring a complete transition on day one, the platform is designed to phase in 800 VDC distribution where it delivers the greatest benefit, helping operators prepare for future generations of accelerated computing without extensive facility redesign.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">The architecture is designed around cluster-level power centers that centralize AC-to-DC conversion and distribute 800 VDC closer to compute resources. The approach simplifies downstream power delivery, supports modular expansion and creates repeatable building blocks for future AI factory growth.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">K-1 also reflects the system-level design philosophy behind NVIDIA AI factories, where power, cooling and computing are engineered together. With support for liquid-cooled infrastructure, scalable power distribution and future Vera Rubin-class deployments, the project demonstrates how ecosystem partners are translating NVIDIA’s AI factory architecture into deployable infrastructure designed for the next era of accelerated computing.</span></p>

<h2 id="meta" class="wp-block-heading" style="font-size: 24px;"><b>Meta and NVIDIA Advance AI Networking With Spectrum-6 and Multiplane Technology </b><b></b><a href="https://blogs.nvidia.com/blog/ocp-global-summit-vera-rubin-scale-ai-factories/#meta"><b><img src="https://s.w.org/images/core/emoji/17.0.2/72x72/1f517.png" alt="🔗" class="wp-smiley" style="height: 1em; max-height: 1em;" /></b></a></h2>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">At the event, </span><span style="font-weight: 400;">Meta </span><span style="font-weight: 400;">and NVIDIA announced that </span><span style="font-weight: 400;">Meta </span><span style="font-weight: 400;">is adopting NVIDIA Spectrum-6 102.4Tb/s Ethernet switches to build larger, higher-performance NVIDIA Vera Rubin AI factories on open networking. </span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Spectrum-6 delivers twice the switching capacity and per-lane bandwidth of Spectrum-4, and provides a lossless Ethernet AI fabric at 200G per lane, with high effective bandwidth and performance isolation that keeps one job’s traffic from slowing another’s.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Meta’s </span><span style="font-weight: 400;">Spectrum-6-based switch, N-IceCube, slots into IcePack, Meta’s 100T, liquid-cooled rack. N-IceCube runs the Facebook Open Switching System (FBOSS), Meta’s open source network operating system that contributed to OCP to support efficient networking at hyperscale. </span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">FBOSS is built on the Switch Abstraction Interface, an OCP standard NVIDIA helped develop, providing a vendor-independent way of controlling switch hardware. Together, these technologies support agentic AI factories of more than 100,000 GPUs that train and serve massive </span><a target="_blank" href="https://www.nvidia.com/en-us/glossary/mixture-of-experts/"><span style="font-weight: 400;">mixture-of-experts</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;"> models.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">The announcement marks the next step in a collaboration that combines Meta’s experience operating AI infrastructure at hyperscale with NVIDIA’s networking technology.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">At the heart of this collaboration is Spectrum-X Multiplane technology, enabled by Spectrum-6 switches and ConnectX-9 SuperNICs. Multiplane distributes GPU traffic in hardware across up to eight independent network planes, enabling AI factories to scale beyond 100,000 GPUs with fewer switch tiers and lower infrastructure costs. Multiple paths between GPUs improve network utilization and resilience: when a component fails, traffic shifts to the remaining planes, keeping GPUs productive and AI applications running, even at reduced bandwidth.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Meta and NVIDIA worked together to strengthen that resilience through deployment experience and rapid engineering iteration. The teams validated improvements across on-premises and cloud environments. </span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">“We are excited about our close partnership with NVIDIA in making multiplane fabrics operationally scalable and performant, a critical requirement both for our own infrastructure builds and for our joint cloud partners,” said Omar Baldonado, senior director of data center and AI networking at Meta. “Extending FBOSS to Spectrum ASICs means we can run that same software stack across our fabrics, and Meta’s new Spectrum-based switch, N-IceCube, brings that into Icepack – our liquid-cooled 102.4T network rack for scale-out that connects upcoming generations of accelerators in our fleet.”</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Together, Meta and NVIDIA are developing an architecture and operational playbook that other AI infrastructure operators can adopt, turning lessons from demanding production workloads into more scalable, resilient and efficient AI factories.</span></p>

<h2 id="usfp" class="wp-block-heading" style="font-size: 24px;"><b>With USFP, NVIDIA and Cisco Reinvent Pluggable Optics for AI Factory Scale </b><b></b><a href="https://blogs.nvidia.com/blog/ocp-global-summit-vera-rubin-scale-ai-factories/#usfp"><b><img src="https://s.w.org/images/core/emoji/17.0.2/72x72/1f517.png" alt="🔗" class="wp-smiley" style="height: 1em; max-height: 1em;" /></b></a></h2>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">As AI factories scale to support larger models and surging inference demand, every component of the infrastructure stack must deliver more performance, efficiency and simplicity — right down to the network connection itself. </span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">At OCP, </span><a target="_blank" href="https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/nvidia-and-cisco-introduce-new-pluggable-optics-form-factor-to-scale-ai-factories"><span style="font-weight: 400;">NVIDIA and </span><span style="font-weight: 400;">Cisco </span><span style="font-weight: 400;">announced plans</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;"> for USFP, a new optical pluggable transceiver module form factor designed to help AI factory builders scale next-generation AI fabrics while improving bandwidth density and power efficiency.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">With approximately the same footprint as the widely adopted 200Gb/s-per-lane OSFP form factor, USFP will provide a path to 400Gb/s-per-lane networking and will extend a transceiver-based deployment model already proven across generations of data center networks. Packing 16 full-duplex lanes with plans to scale up to 32 lanes, USFP will deliver four times the bandwidth density of traditional 200Gb/s-per-lane pluggable optics. </span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">The result is the ability to connect more compute resources without adding network tiers that can increase latency, power consumption and complexity.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">The new form factor is designed for the scale and efficiency demands of next generation AI factories, supporting higher-bandwidth fabrics while preserving the flexibility and serviceability that have made pluggable optics a mainstay of modern data centers. It also will deliver roughly 1.3x greater power efficiency, helping operators move more data with less energy.</span><span style="font-weight: 400;">   </span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">To help drive an open ecosystem, NVIDIA and </span><span style="font-weight: 400;">Cisco </span><span style="font-weight: 400;">are collaborating with</span><span style="font-weight: 400;"> TE Connectivity </span><span style="font-weight: 400;">and </span><span style="font-weight: 400;">Amphenol </span><span style="font-weight: 400;">on cages, connectors and modules, with plans to advance USFP through a community-led, multi-source effort. Together, the companies are laying the foundation for more scalable, efficient and interoperable AI factory networking.</span></p>

<h2 id="google-falcon" class="wp-block-heading" style="font-size: 24px;"><b>Google Falcon Transport Supports NVIDIA ConnectX-10 for Interoperable AI Networking </b><b></b><a href="https://blogs.nvidia.com/blog/ocp-global-summit-vera-rubin-scale-ai-factories/#google-falcon"><b><img src="https://s.w.org/images/core/emoji/17.0.2/72x72/1f517.png" alt="🔗" class="wp-smiley" style="height: 1em; max-height: 1em;" /></b></a></h2>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">NVIDIA and </span><span style="font-weight: 400;">Google </span><span style="font-weight: 400;">also announced Falcon Transport support on future NVIDIA ConnectX-10 SuperNICs and BlueField DPUs, helping improve network efficiency for large-scale AI infrastructure. </span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Falcon is a transport protocol designed to deliver data reliably over Ethernet networks, which can otherwise drop packets under congestion. It supports AI and storage workloads through multipath load balancing, programmable congestion control, hardware loss recovery and per-connection security.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">This collaboration combines NVIDIA hardware acceleration with DOCA software to implement Falcon and extend NVIDIA networking to</span><span style="font-weight: 400;"> Google’s </span><span style="font-weight: 400;">data center transport. NVIDIA and </span><span style="font-weight: 400;">Google </span><span style="font-weight: 400;">NICs can communicate over a common transport while applications retain standard RDMA programming interfaces.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">By distributing traffic across paths, adapting to congestion, recovering dropped packets and protecting connections, Falcon helps workloads make better use of available bandwidth and maintain predictable performance as Ethernet fabrics scale.</span></p>

										<content:encoded><![CDATA[<div id="bsf_rt_marker"></div><p><span style="font-weight: 400;">At a Microsoft event in San Francisco on Wednesday, NVIDIA founder and CEO Jensen Huang and Microsoft CEO Satya Nadella outlined how NVIDIA and Microsoft are co-engineering hardware and software for AI agents to run on Windows PCs.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">NVIDIA was founded because of Windows, Huang said. Now AI agents are coming to Windows.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">“If you look at the entire journey of our company, Windows was at the core of it,” Huang said, tracing back NVIDIA’s support for Windows and Microsoft over several decades. “If not for Windows there would be no GeForce.”  </span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Huang connected that history — and his long-term vision — to the agentic era Wednesday during a fireside chat with Nadella hosted by Sriram Krishnan, a former senior White House policy advisor on AI at the Windows AI and Surface event, held at Dogpatch Studios in San Francisco.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">“The thing that I really give Jensen all the credit for, quite frankly, is that consistency of vision of what this can be—not today, not tomorrow, but as a secular trend,” Nadella said. “And that’s what brings us to this moment.” </span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">“The personal computer is the ultimate tool, it’s my ultimate tool, and for a whole generation of people, it’s our ultimate tool,” Huang said. “What happens in the era of agents, when the agent is on your computer? It is now your personal assistant.” </span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">The event opened with Nadella before Pavan Davuluri, executive vice president of Windows and Devices at Microsoft, walked through the Windows updates designed for the agentic era.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Turning Windows into a secure platform for agents, Microsoft announced general availability of Microsoft Execution Containers (MXC), the OS-level infrastructure that lets agents run safely and persistently in the background, under operating system control.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">“We are building these primitives directly into Windows, so agents can be secured, observed and governed,” Davuluri explained. “With MXC, Microsoft Security and Agent 365, we’re unlocking the full potential of agents on Windows.”</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Nadella returned to that theme in his talk with Huang. “We needed to make the desktop the most secure place for agents to execute,” Nadella said. </span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">“What Satya just said is going to be the foundation of the next generation of IT as we know it,” Huang said. “Just as Windows and DirectX revolutionized how applications were built, MXC is going to revolutionize how agents are built and deployed.” </span></p>
<h2><b>A New Beginning for Windows PCs: Preorder RTX Spark Laptops Today</b></h2>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Among the announcements Huang and Nadella outlined Wednesday, RTX Spark puts the full NVIDIA AI stack into Windows laptops and compact desktops, with laptop preorders open today and available Friday, Oct. 16. Compact desktops will be available for sale in November.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Windows is bringing local AI closer to everyday work, while new hardware gives developers room to run increasingly capable models right on their PC. Introducing Surface Laptop Ultra, Davuluri tied that vision to NVIDIA RTX Spark.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">“We built Surface Laptop Ultra around NVIDIA RTX Spark,” Davuluri said. “With up to 128 gigs of unified memory and up to a petaflop of AI compute, you can run models on this laptop that simply don’t fit on a traditional machine.” </span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Systems are coming from </span><span style="font-weight: 400;">Acer, ASUS, Dell, HP, Lenovo, Microsoft, MSI and Gigabyte</span><span style="font-weight: 400;"> with designs ranging from slim laptops to compact desktops built for always-on agents. </span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">RTX Spark combines an NVIDIA Blackwell RTX GPU with up to 6,144 cores, and an up to 20-core NVIDIA Grace CPU connected at 600 GB/s. </span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">One petaflop of FP4 AI performance and up to 128GB unified memory makes RTX Spark effective for local AI. It can run models such as Qwen 3.8 Flash Next, a 125B model with 51B n-gram that matches the intelligence of many cloud models, unmetered and without sending data to the cloud.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">RTX Spark runs the full NVIDIA CUDA platform — the same software stack that runs across NVIDIA hardware — and is built for every way people use a PC:</span></p>
<ul>
<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><b>Developers</b><span style="font-weight: 400;"> can move models, tools and workflows without rewriting, running the same NVIDIA AI stack from RTX Spark to DGX Station.</span></li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><b>Creators</b><span style="font-weight: 400;"> get 5th-generation Tensor Cores with NVFP4 support, hardware-accelerated AV1, and 4:2:2 video encode and decode, DLSS and RTX ray tracing across the full production pipeline.</span></li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><b>Gamers</b><span style="font-weight: 400;"> can run AAA games at 1440p over 100 frames per second with DLSS 5, Reflex and G-SYNC. </span></li>
</ul>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">The compact desktop configuration puts the same RTX Spark superchip in a small chassis designed for 24/7 operation — a dedicated local AI system that keeps agents running continuously. </span><a target="_blank" href="https://www.nvidia.com/en-us/products/rtx-spark/"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Preorder RTX Spark today</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;">.</span></p>
<h2><b>NVIDIA DGX Station for Windows: Frontier AI Compute on the Enterprise Desktop</b></h2>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">The event previewed NVIDIA DGX Station for Windows today — the first deskside AI supercomputer to bring GB300 Grace Blackwell-class AI infrastructure directly into the Windows ecosystem.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">“This unlocks the power to run frontier-class models locally,” </span><span style="font-weight: 400;">Davuluri</span><span style="font-weight: 400;"> said. “Capabilities that once required renting a cluster, now in a deskside supercomputer.” </span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Until now, DGX Station ran on Linux — which meant enterprise developers maintained two separate environments: Linux for heavy AI workloads and Windows for the productivity tools, applications and workflows. </span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">The vast majority of Fortune 500 companies are standardized on Windows, and that gap has cost developers time and resources — developers either moved to Linux to access AI compute, or stayed in Windows with limited hardware options for heavy-duty model development and multi-agent workloads.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">NVIDIA DGX Station for Windows runs on the GB300 Grace Blackwell Ultra Desktop Superchip, delivering 748GB of coherent memory and up to 20 petaFLOPS of FP4 AI compute — enough to run models up to a trillion-parameter scale locally. </span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Teams of developers and researchers at AI-native companies, leading research labs and enterprises can build and run always-on AI agents that connect directly to the Windows applications and infrastructure they already use, and fine-tune and inference large models without leaving their primary machine. Linux AI toolchains remain accessible through WSL when needed.</span></p>

<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">For operators, the strategic value of open models is fivefold:</span></p>
<ol>
<li><span style="font-weight: 400;">They expand access to </span><a target="_blank" href="https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/nvidia-launches-nemotron-coalition-of-leading-global-ai-labs-to-advance-open-frontier-models"><span style="font-weight: 400;">frontier‑level intelligence</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;"> at lower cost, allowing operators to reserve closed models for the workloads where they drive the most value. Independent benchmarks such as the </span><a target="_blank" href="https://artificialanalysis.ai/evaluations/artificial-analysis-intelligence-index"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index v4.3.2</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;"> show that leading open models are becoming more competitive across demanding reasoning, coding, scientific and agentic workloads.</span></li>
<li><span style="font-weight: 400;">They support telco‑specific customization, with open weights and training recipes that operators can fine‑tune for their own operations using network, customer and industry data.</span></li>
<li><span style="font-weight: 400;">They enable </span><a href="https://blogs.nvidia.com/blog/what-is-trustworthy-ai/"><span style="font-weight: 400;">trustworthy AI</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;"> by giving telcos greater visibility into and control over model artifacts and behavior, so models can be evaluated, adapted and governed in alignment with regulations and business policies.</span></li>
<li><span style="font-weight: 400;">They enable flexible, secure deployment: teams can size and optimize open models to run across public clouds, private infrastructure and edge environments.</span></li>
<li><span style="font-weight: 400;"><span style="font-weight: 400;">They unlock the opportunity for telcos to deliver locally adapted AI services to enterprise and government customers by hosting and fine-tuning open models.</span></span></li>
</ol>
<p>&nbsp;</p>
<h2><strong>Open Foundations, Tuned for Telecom Operations</strong></h2>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">The </span><a target="_blank" href="https://www.nvidia.com/en-us/ai-data-science/foundation-models/nemotron/"><span style="font-weight: 400;">NVIDIA Nemotron</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;"> family of open models provides </span><a target="_blank" href="https://www.nvidia.com/en-us/glossary/frontier-models/"><span style="font-weight: 400;">frontier</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;">-level reasoning performance optimized for agentic workflows, as well as speech capabilities for voice applications, with open weights, training data and recipes. </span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">SoftBank Corp. illustrates how an operator can use open models as a basis for developing and continuously advancing its telecom-specific AI capabilities.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">“Open models allow SoftBank Corp. to build on the rapid progress of global foundation models while applying the network knowledge and operational expertise we have accumulated over many years,” said Rajeev Koodli, principal fellow of SoftBank Corp. and senior vice president of SB Telecom America. “We are using open foundations extensively, including NVIDIA Nemotron models and others, in developing our SoftBank Large Telecom Model, continuously advancing it for telecom-specific use cases such as network operations, design and overall management.”</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">In addition, NVIDIA is collaborating with partners to help turn open models into practical building blocks for telecom AI.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">NVIDIA </span><a href="https://blogs.nvidia.com/blog/nvidia-agentic-ai-blueprints-telco-reasoning-models/"><span style="font-weight: 400;">announced</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;"> the 30-billion-parameter </span><a href="https://blogs.nvidia.com/blog/nvidia-agentic-ai-blueprints-telco-reasoning-models/"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Nemotron 3 Large Telco Model</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;"> (LTM), </span><a target="_blank" href="https://adaptkey.ai/blog/building-domain-expert-llms"><span style="font-weight: 400;">fine-tuned</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;"> by </span><a target="_blank" href="https://huggingface.co/AdaptKey/AdaptKey-Nemotron-30b"><span style="font-weight: 400;">AdaptKey</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;"> on open source telecom datasets to deliver accuracy gains for telecom-specific tasks. This gives operators an open baseline that can understand telecom industry terminology and reason effectively through telecom operations workflows such as network configuration and customer incident triage.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">To help operators customize the Nemotron 3 LTM and other open models with their own operational data, NVIDIA released the </span><a target="_blank" href="https://nvidia-nemo.github.io/Skills/tutorials/2026/02/27/teaching-a-model-to-reason-over-telecom-network-incidents/"><span style="font-weight: 400;">full recipe</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;"> that walks through the end-to-end fine-tuning pipeline for adapting open models to operator-specific networks, customers and procedures using </span><a target="_blank" href="https://github.com/nvidia-nemo"><span style="font-weight: 400;">NVIDIA NeMo</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;"> open libraries. </span></p>
<h2><b>Scale Open Models Into Production Workflows</b></h2>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Open models are a critical building block, but it takes more than models to bring autonomous telecom operations safely into production.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">For AT&amp;T, the value of model choice lies in making that flexibility operational in alignment with its business priorities.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">“At AT&amp;T, we believe the future of AI is not about choosing a single model; it’s about intelligently matching every workload to the right combination of performance, cost and control,” said Andy Markus, chief data and AI officer of AT&amp;T. “Open models are essential to that approach, and with NVIDIA, we can bring that model-choice strategy into production with the scale, reliability and governance our business requires.”</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Operators need pipelines that prepare and protect their data for model fine‑tuning by anonymizing sensitive records and generating privacy‑preserving synthetic datasets. They also need a platform that can turn open models into governed, autonomous agentic workflows.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">NVIDIA provides that </span><a target="_blank" href="https://developer.nvidia.com/blog/how-telcos-build-autonomous-networks-with-agentic-ai"><span style="font-weight: 400;">end-to-end platform</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;">, powered by </span><a target="_blank" href="https://www.nvidia.com/en-us/data-center/products/ai-enterprise/"><span style="font-weight: 400;">NVIDIA AI Enterprise</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;"> software and </span><a href="https://blogs.nvidia.com/blog/nvidia-agent-toolkit-open-models-tools-skills-secure-runtime-ai-agents/?ncid=so-nvsh-718272-vt26"><span style="font-weight: 400;">NVIDIA Agent Toolkit</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;">, and spanning data pipelines, open models, agent orchestration, secure runtimes and simulation.  </span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Together with a strong </span><a href="https://blogs.nvidia.com/blog/telecom-ai-agents-dtw-ignite-2026/"><span style="font-weight: 400;">partner ecosystem</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;"> building at every layer of this platform, telecom operators have a clear path to translate the benefits of open models into production-ready AI workflows.</span></p>
<h2><strong>Create a Platform for Local AI Innovation</strong></h2>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">As telecom operators </span><a target="_blank" href="https://resources.nvidia.com/en-us-telco-ai-factories/ebook-sovereign-ai-factories"><span style="font-weight: 400;">build AI infrastructure</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;"> aligned with </span><a href="https://blogs.nvidia.com/blog/nations-deploy-ai-strategic-priorities/"><span style="font-weight: 400;">national AI strategies</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;">, open models are giving them a foundation to deliver AI services tailored to local languages, industries, regulations and data governance requirements. </span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Operators across countries can fine-tune open models for local-language and industry-specific needs, as well as host open models on their trusted platforms that customers can consume directly or build on with their own data and applications.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">For Indosat Ooredoo Hutchison, one of Indonesia&#8217;s largest telecom operators, that means building AI that reflects the country&#8217;s own language and culture through its Sahabat-AI family of open source models.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">“For countries like Indonesia, the value of open models goes beyond access to powerful AI. It is about adapting that intelligence to our own language, culture, data and real-world needs,” said Chirag Sukhadia, chief data and AI officer of Indosat Ooredoo Hutchison. “Sahabat-AI puts this into practice, using open models as a foundation to build AI that understands Indonesia and can be developed for local applications. This allows us not only to adopt AI, but to build capabilities around it and enable more Indonesians to create with AI on their own terms.”</span></p>
