										<content:encoded><![CDATA[<div id="bsf_rt_marker"></div><p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Kazakhstan’s AI ecosystem is moving from infrastructure plans to deployment. </span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">The shift is visible across the full AI stack — from infrastructure to models to applications.</span></p>
<p>The announcements below — coinciding with a visit to Astana by NVIDIA founder and CEO Jensen Huang — point to the nation’s increasing supply of accelerated computing, along with its growing demand for what comes next.</p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">On Friday, Oct. 23, NVIDIA founder and CEO Jensen Huang visited Astana, meeting with President Kassym-Jomart Tokayev at the Akorda Presidential Palace and joining Deputy Prime Minister Zhaslan Madiyev — Kazakhstan’s minister of AI and digital development — for a public fireside chat at the </span><a target="_blank" href="http://alem.ai"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Alem.AI</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;"> program. </span></p>
<div style="margin: 0 !important; padding: 0 !important; text-align: center; line-height: 1;"><a style="display: inline-flex; align-items: center; justify-content: center; margin: 0 !important; padding: 10px !important; background-color: #76b900; color: #ffffff !important; font-family: 'NVIDIA Sans', Arial, sans-serif; font-size: 16px; font-weight: bold; line-height: 1 !important; text-align: center; text-decoration: none !important; white-space: nowrap; vertical-align: top;" href="https://blogs.nvidia.com/gtc-berlin-2026-news/">Read All NVIDIA in Europe Announcements</a></div>
<h2></h2>
<h2><img fetchpriority="high" decoding="async" class="alignnone size-full wp-image-99469" src="https://blogs.nvidia.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/S-31-edit-scaled.jpg" alt="" width="2048" height="1366" srcset="https://blogs.nvidia.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/S-31-edit-scaled.jpg 2048w, https://blogs.nvidia.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/S-31-edit-960x640.jpg 960w, https://blogs.nvidia.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/S-31-edit-1680x1121.jpg 1680w, https://blogs.nvidia.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/S-31-edit-1280x854.jpg 1280w, https://blogs.nvidia.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/S-31-edit-1536x1025.jpg 1536w, https://blogs.nvidia.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/S-31-edit-630x420.jpg 630w" sizes="(max-width: 2048px) 100vw, 2048px" /></h2>
<h2><b>Jensen Huang and Kazakhstan’s AI Minister Speak at Alem.AI</b></h2>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">In their fireside chat at Alem.AI, Kazakhstan’s international center for artificial intelligence, Madiyev and Huang discussed the country’s push to become an AI nation — building the energy and computing infrastructure to support homegrown AI and applying it across the economy.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Madiyev said Kazakhstan wants AI that reflects its language and culture. “We want AI that understands and speaks Kazakh and captures cultural contexts and cultural specifics,” he said, describing the country’s work on Kazakh-language models. </span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Huang said Kazakhstan could turn its energy resources into a foundation for AI production — and a new kind of export. “Instead of just exporting energy, in the future, you could transform energy into intelligence and export intelligence,” he said.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">He also argued that local data is central to that ambition: it embodies a country’s culture, history and values. “Your country’s data is your natural resource. It belongs to you.” </span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">The conversation also turned to physical AI. Madiyev pointed to Kazakhstan’s factories, mines, agriculture, transport and logistics as potential areas for development, asking how the country could become a hub for training models for robots and other physical systems.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Huang called the idea “fantastic,” saying physical AI could transform industries from mining and mineral processing to agriculture. </span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Huang said AI and robotics can help people do more, but ambition will shape how fully they seize that opportunity. He applied the idea to Kazakhstan, saying AI could help lift the country and its people.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">“If you have ambition, and I believe you do, AI will lift your nation,” Huang said. “AI will lift your people.”</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Huang also praised the founders of Higgsfield AI, the Kazakhstan-founded startup, and it was announced NVIDIA is making an investment into the startup during the event. “The first thing and the last thing is you invest in people, not companies,” he said, describing the founders as visionary and pointing to their effort to compete globally. </span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Asked about AI security, Huang said the technology must be developed and deployed responsibly. He described NVIDIA’s OpenShell as a way to contain AI agents by limiting their access to files and tools and monitoring their activity.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;"><img decoding="async" class="alignnone size-full wp-image-99486" src="https://blogs.nvidia.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/S-17-edit-scaled.jpeg" alt="" width="2048" height="1152" srcset="https://blogs.nvidia.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/S-17-edit-scaled.jpeg 2048w, https://blogs.nvidia.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/S-17-edit-960x540.jpeg 960w, https://blogs.nvidia.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/S-17-edit-1680x945.jpeg 1680w, https://blogs.nvidia.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/S-17-edit-1280x720.jpeg 1280w, https://blogs.nvidia.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/S-17-edit-1536x864.jpeg 1536w, https://blogs.nvidia.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/S-17-edit-1290x725.jpeg 1290w, https://blogs.nvidia.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/S-17-edit-630x354.jpeg 630w, https://blogs.nvidia.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/S-17-edit-300x169.jpeg 300w, https://blogs.nvidia.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/S-17-edit-400x225.jpeg 400w" sizes="(max-width: 2048px) 100vw, 2048px" /></span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">For young people entering a fast-changing field, Huang recommended learning how AI works — not just how to use it. He said aspiring AI programmers should understand the technology’s principles, strengths and weaknesses, combining technical knowledge with the ability to work effectively with AI. </span></p>
<p>“I envy being 18 years old today… because the world is changing so rapidly, and there’s a complete reset because of technology.”</p>
<h2><b>The Infrastructure Layer Is Scaling<br />
</b></h2>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">The visits coincided with a signing ceremony marking Firebird’s investment agreement with the government of Kazakhstan, held at the presidential palace. </span><b></b></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Building on its first AI factory in Armenia, </span><span style="font-weight: 400;">Firebird</span><span style="font-weight: 400;"> is expanding into Kazakhstan, where it has secured a U.S. export license for 45,000 NVIDIA Grace Blackwell GPUs. The first phase is under construction with Kazakhtelecom at Data Center Valley, targeting commercial launch in the third quarter of 2027.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">A planned second phase would bring Firebird’s total potential AI infrastructure capacity in Kazakhstan to approximately 500 megawatts. Firebird also plans to establish Firebird Labs Kazakhstan to support local AI startups, research and talent development through funding, access to compute and research partnerships.</span></p>
<p>Firebird cofounder and CEO Razmig Hovaghimian attended the signing ceremony at the Akorda Presidential Palace alongside government officials.</p>
<p>“What began as a seed of an idea 180 days ago has grown into a major investment in Kazakhstan’s AI future,” said Razmig Hovaghimian, co-Founder and CEO of Firebird. “President Tokayev’s vision gave us the confidence to think bigger about what we can build together. With Jensen Huang here today, that ambition feels more tangible than ever. We’re building the infrastructure for Kazakhstan’s talent and ideas to reach the world.”</p>
<p><a target="_blank" href="https://telecom.kz/en"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Kazakhtelecom</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;">, the nation’s largest telecommunications company, has already deployed 400 NVIDIA H200 and 480 NVIDIA B300 GPUs in Astana and Almaty. </span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Next year, the company plans to deploy more than 1,000 GPUs in the <a target="_blank" href="https://dc-valley.com/en">Data Center Valley</a>, along with more than 1,000 NVIDIA Vera Rubin GPUs in the Ekibastuz region, serving diverse workloads with infrastructure that enterprises, developers and institutions can use at scale.</span></p>
<h2><b>An Honorary Black Belt for Huang</b></h2>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Later that day, also at the Akorda Presidential Palace, President Kassym-Jomart Tokayev presented Huang with an honorary black belt in Judo. The belt, embroidered in gold stitching, was a special thanks from President Tokayev for Huang’s support of Kazakhstan’s AI ambition and for leadership aligned with the spirit and rigor of the martial art.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">At the end of their meeting President Tokayev had another honor for Huang, presenting him with the Order of Dostyk, First Class, for Huang&#8217;s contribution to the development of artificial intelligence in Kazakhstan. <em>Dostyk</em> means friendship and the Order honors peace, friendship and cooperation.</span></p>
<h2><b>AI-Native Companies Scale With Compute</b></h2>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">And that development is accelerating. The nation’s AI-native companies are also building out their compute capacity — and the applications that run atop them.<br />
</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Higgsfield is expanding its AI infrastructure with NVIDIA Vera Rubin running on cloud service providers, giving the company additional capacity as models, products and customer demand for its generative media applications grow.</span></p>
<p><a target="_blank" href="https://blog.indrive.com/article/indrive-and-pony-ai-announce-collaboration-to-explore-autonomous-mobility-in-kazakhstan-built-on-nvidia-hyperion"><span style="font-weight: 400;">inDrive and Pony.ai</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;"> are also collaborating to explore robotaxi services in Kazakhstan, with Astana as the first deployment city. The companies will assess the feasibility of bringing Pony.ai’s seventh-generation robotaxis to the inDrive app, supported by NVIDIA AI infrastructure, built on the </span><a target="_blank" href="https://www.nvidia.com/en-us/solutions/autonomous-vehicles/"><span style="font-weight: 400;">NVIDIA Hyperion</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;"> sensor and compute platform with the </span><a target="_blank" href="https://www.nvidia.com/en-us/ai-trust-center/halos/autonomous-vehicles/"><span style="font-weight: 400;">NVIDIA Halos</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;"> safety system.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">More local capacity gives developers and companies room to train, fine-tune and serve increasingly capable models. Successful applications, in turn, create demand for more compute.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Kazakhstan’s AI opportunity isn’t defined by GPU counts alone. Connecting that infrastructure with developers, universities, startups and industries can turn accelerated computing into useful applications.</span></p>
<p><img decoding="async" class="alignnone size-full wp-image-99481" src="https://blogs.nvidia.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/OLZH9727-1-scaled.jpg" alt="" width="2048" height="1152" srcset="https://blogs.nvidia.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/OLZH9727-1-scaled.jpg 2048w, https://blogs.nvidia.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/OLZH9727-1-960x540.jpg 960w, https://blogs.nvidia.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/OLZH9727-1-1680x945.jpg 1680w, https://blogs.nvidia.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/OLZH9727-1-1280x720.jpg 1280w, https://blogs.nvidia.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/OLZH9727-1-1536x864.jpg 1536w, https://blogs.nvidia.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/OLZH9727-1-1290x725.jpg 1290w, https://blogs.nvidia.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/OLZH9727-1-630x354.jpg 630w, https://blogs.nvidia.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/OLZH9727-1-300x169.jpg 300w, https://blogs.nvidia.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/OLZH9727-1-400x225.jpg 400w" sizes="(max-width: 2048px) 100vw, 2048px" /></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">That was at the core of the discussion the NVIDIA team had with hundreds of developers from across Kazakhstan during a reception later in the evening. The gathering in this self-proclaimed AI nation was inspiring because of the ideas shared, the clear drive to build and a collective ambition to share it with the world. </span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Today’s announcements </span><span style="font-weight: 400;">represent different layers of the AI stack in Kazakhstan— infrastructure deployment, access to compute and AI-native product development — where the AI foundation is reaching new scale.</span></p>

										<content:encoded><![CDATA[<div id="bsf_rt_marker"></div><p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Launched today, </span><a target="_blank" href="https://www.nvidia.com/en-us/networking/products/software/minerva/"><span style="font-weight: 400;">NVIDIA Minerva</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;"> is an AIOps platform built to help teams find problems earlier and keep AI factories productive. It brings together telemetry from infrastructure and AI workloads, then applies purpose-built AI models to turn that data into timely, actionable insight.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">AI factories are among the most complex operations in the world, with vast numbers of GPUs working across high-speed networks to train models and serve AI applications. At that scale, a single degraded component can slow an entire job. The warning signs may be easy to miss, such as a GPU running hotter than its peers or an NVMe drive losing performance under load.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">The platform focuses on AI factory yield, a measure of the useful work infrastructure produces through GPU hours, goodput, cluster uptime and token production.</span></p>
<div style="margin: 0 !important; padding: 0 !important; text-align: center; line-height: 1;"><a style="display: inline-flex; align-items: center; justify-content: center; margin: 0 !important; padding: 10px !important; background-color: #76b900; color: #ffffff !important; font-family: 'NVIDIA Sans', Arial, sans-serif; font-size: 16px; font-weight: bold; line-height: 1 !important; text-align: center; text-decoration: none !important; white-space: nowrap; vertical-align: top;" href="https://blogs.nvidia.com/gtc-berlin-2026-news/">Read All NVIDIA in Europe Announcements</a></div>
<p>&nbsp;</p>
<h2><b>Delivering a Dynamic View of an AI Factory</b></h2>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Traditional monitoring tools often divide the data center into separate views. Networking, compute, site reliability and machine learning teams each see a different part of the operation. Minerva connects those signals across NVIDIA GPUs, networking, storage, schedulers and workloads.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">With these signals, Minerva builds a dynamic knowledge graph that logically maps the infrastructure — GPUs, SuperNICs, switches and cables — and the jobs running on it. This context helps operators trace a slow training job to the component affecting it, even across a factory with hundreds of thousands of GPUs.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">It analyzes billions of data points per hour using supervised and unsupervised machine learning. It learns normal behavior, detects previously unseen anomalies, predicts emerging failures and correlates related events based on the factory’s topology. Instead of sending every symptom to an operator, it can group an event storm into a focused incident with a likely root cause.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Minerva comes with a native AI assistant and connects to external agentic AI tools. This adds a natural-language interface grounded in live telemetry. Operators can investigate cluster health and receive recommended remediation through conversation. Teams can keep a person in the loop to manually implement fixes or connect Minerva to automation through infrastructure application programming interfaces and external systems.</span></p>
<h2><strong>Results That Maximize AI Factory Yield</strong></h2>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">The ability to correlate workload and infrastructure issues along with its agentic AI capabilities help simplify AI factory operations. Instead of dealing with a blizzard of alerts, Minerva aggregates related problems into a single actionable item. This allows AIOps teams to move from reactive to proactive maintenance. And all of this helps AI factories maximize GPU uptime.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Minerva delivers value for the key metrics AI factories focus on:</span></p>
<ul>
<li><b>Longer time between interruptions</b><span style="font-weight: 400;">: Minerva spots trouble before it happens, so jobs run longer without incident.</span></li>
<li><b>Faster time to repair</b><span style="font-weight: 400;">: When an incident does happen, Minerva pinpoints the problem, leading to a significantly quicker resolution.</span></li>
<li><b>Greater insight</b><span style="font-weight: 400;">: Modern AI infrastructure generates a flood of data; Minerva filters it out, leaving only the handful of alerts that actually matter.</span></li>
</ul>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">IREN is among the NVIDIA partners using Minerva to optimize its AI factories. </span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">“As we scale our AI factories, the key to providing the best service for our customers is maximizing the productivity of our GPUs,” said </span><span style="font-weight: 400;">Denis Skrinnikoff, chief technology officer of IREN. </span><span style="font-weight: 400;">“NVIDIA Minerva gives our operations team one view across the network, the GPUs and the jobs running on them, so we focus more on proactive maintenance rather than troubleshooting. That visibility strengthens our operating model for the infrastructure we’re building next.”</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Joining</span> <span style="font-weight: 400;">IREN</span><span style="font-weight: 400;">, </span><span style="font-weight: 400;">Aolani</span><span style="font-weight: 400;">, </span><a target="_blank" href="https://www.bitdeer.ai/en/blog/nvidia-minerva/"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Bitdeer AI</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;">, </span><span style="font-weight: 400;">IBM</span><span style="font-weight: 400;">, </span><span style="font-weight: 400;">Jio,</span> <span style="font-weight: 400;">Lambda</span><span style="font-weight: 400;">, </span><span style="font-weight: 400;">Quake AI </span><span style="font-weight: 400;">and </span><span style="font-weight: 400;">Tesla </span><span style="font-weight: 400;">plan to deploy Minerva. </span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">In addition to</span> <span style="font-weight: 400;">Dell Technologies</span><span style="font-weight: 400;">, </span><a target="_blank" href="https://news.lenovo.com/europes-ai-moment-gtc-berlin-26"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Lenovo </span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;">and </span><a target="_blank" href="https://learn-more.supermicro.com/data-center-stories/supermicro-nvidia-minerva-supercloud-ai-factory"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Supermicro </span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;">will offer Minerva as part of their AI factory operations portfolios. </span></p>

										<content:encoded><![CDATA[<div id="bsf_rt_marker"></div><p><span style="font-weight: 400;">At GTC Berlin, banks, payment companies and financial data providers are showing what production-scale intelligence looks like — built on NVIDIA accelerated computing, powered by open weight models and running on their own infrastructure.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Revolut</span><span style="font-weight: 400;"> is rebuilding its technology stack on a single foundation model </span><a target="_blank" href="https://resources.nvidia.com/en-us-financial-services-industry/revolut-builds-a-transaction-foundation-model-1185435"><span style="font-weight: 400;">PRAGMA</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;">, powered by NVIDIA accelerated computing. Using the </span><a target="_blank" href="https://developer.nvidia.com/topics/ai/data-science/cuda-x-data-science-libraries/cudf"><span style="font-weight: 400;">NVIDIA cuDF</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;"> toolkit and </span><a target="_blank" href="https://developer.nvidia.com/dynamo-triton"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Dynamo-Triton software</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;"> on </span><a target="_blank" href="https://www.nvidia.com/en-us/data-center/technologies/blackwell-architecture/"><span style="font-weight: 400;">NVIDIA Blackwell Ultra GPUs</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;">, Revolut is scaling PRAGMA in production, servicing an initial 80 million customers and engineered to scale 3x.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">“Revolut processes billions of transactions — the opportunity was always there, but the question was whether foundation models could operate at that scale and across domains. PRAGMA answers that,” said Pavel Nesterov, head of AI at Revolut. “Built in collaboration with NVIDIA, it’s the clearest proof yet that the gap between a research breakthrough and a production banking system can be closed.”</span></p>

<h2><b>Training on Accelerated Infrastructure</b></h2>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Starling Bank</span><span style="font-weight: 400;">, the first bank to train an agentic model on U.K. sovereign cloud infrastructure, has developed “Starling Intelligence” — the intelligence layer that underpins its AI capability — on </span><span style="font-weight: 400;">Civo’s</span><span style="font-weight: 400;"> U.K. cloud using the bank’s own transaction data. </span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Lloyds Banking Group</span><span style="font-weight: 400;"> built </span><a target="_blank" href="https://medium.com/ai-at-lloyds-banking-group/introducing-atum-a-foundation-model-for-banking-4d5291577d50"><span style="font-weight: 400;">ATUM</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;">, a transaction foundation model that converts billions of banking transactions into reusable intelligence — accelerated with NVIDIA RTX PRO 6000 Blackwell GPUs and improving efficiency across bank-wide use cases.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">SBS</span><span style="font-weight: 400;"> launched a banking sovereign intelligence platform so banks can run generative and agentic models without their data leaving their own walls.</span></p>
<h2><b>Open Models at a Fraction of the Cost</b></h2>

<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">NatWest</span><span style="font-weight: 400;"> is building technology designed to recognize the full range of British accents, dialects and financial terminology, developed with NVIDIA’s speech models, including Nemotron 3.5 ASR. </span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Modulr</span><span style="font-weight: 400;"> is building specialized models for distinct parts of payment operations, based on the NVIDIA Nemotron-3-Ultra-550B open model.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Santander</span><span style="font-weight: 400;"> is an early adopter of the new NVIDIA Kumo family of open weight foundation models for structured financial data, using them to build specialized intelligence for core banking workloads.</span></p>
<h2><b>Accelerated Computing Transforms Financial Research</b></h2>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">LSEG</span><span style="font-weight: 400;"> is working with NVIDIA to help financial institutions analyze large volumes of historical tick data up to 10x faster with the </span><a target="_blank" href="https://docs.pola.rs/user-guide/lazy/gpu/"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Polars GPU engine</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;">, accelerated by NVIDIA cuDF on NVIDIA RTX PRO 6000 Blackwell GPUs. </span></p>

<h2><b>Speeding Data Pipelines to Improve Fraud Detection </b></h2>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">RTX PRO 6000 Blackwell Server Edition GPUs and NVIDIA cuDF help accelerate data processing across multi-terabyte-scale, siloed, regulated sources, compressing the time from data to decision for fraud and other scenarios.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">HSBC</span><span style="font-weight: 400;"> provides near-real-time client insights that support customer processes and on-demand decision-making while minimizing risk and fraud, powered by 5x faster data processing on </span><a target="_blank" href="http://starburst.io/press-release/starburst-introduces-torin-gpu-native-analytics-engine-built-on-nvidia-cudf"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Starburst Enterprise’s GPU Engine</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;">. </span></p>

										<content:encoded><![CDATA[<div id="bsf_rt_marker"></div><p><span style="font-weight: 400;">To help developers rapidly build </span><a target="_blank" href="https://www.nvidia.com/en-us/glossary/world-models/"><span style="font-weight: 400;">world models</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;">, which help </span><a target="_blank" href="https://www.nvidia.com/en-us/glossary/generative-physical-ai/"><span style="font-weight: 400;">physical AI</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;"> systems understand and simulate the real world, NVIDIA has expanded the </span><a target="_blank" href="https://www.nvidia.com/en-us/ai/cosmos/"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Cosmos platform</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;"> with new training tools, simulation checkpoints and Cosmos Data Factory blueprints. </span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Ranking top five on six different open models leaderboards, the NVIDIA Cosmos platform gives world model builders the frontier open models — as well as data, training and optimization tools and recipes — to scale training data, adapt models, test behavior and optimize for performance before deployment. </span></p>
<h2><strong>Build Better Training Data</strong></h2>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Building world models begins with multimodal, multisensory data.</span></p>
<p><a target="_blank" href="https://github.com/NVIDIA/cosmos-data-factory"><span style="font-weight: 400;">NVIDIA Cosmos Data Factory</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;"> brings together the tools and skills for labeling, curating, augmenting and evaluating data at scale for training physical AI. Cosmos Curator, a GPU-accelerated solution for curating video training datasets, has now added support for multimodal multisensory inputs and processing blocks.</span></p>
<div style="margin: 0 !important; padding: 0 !important; text-align: center; line-height: 1;"><a style="display: inline-flex; align-items: center; justify-content: center; margin: 0 !important; padding: 10px !important; background-color: #76b900; color: #ffffff !important; font-family: 'NVIDIA Sans', Arial, sans-serif; font-size: 16px; font-weight: bold; line-height: 1 !important; text-align: center; text-decoration: none !important; white-space: nowrap; vertical-align: top;" href="https://blogs.nvidia.com/gtc-berlin-2026-news/">Read All NVIDIA in Europe Announcements</a></div>
<p>&nbsp;</p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Cosmos Data Factory now offers the following five cloud-native blueprints and is available through </span><a target="_blank" href="https://github.com/NVIDIA/cosmos-data-factory"><span style="font-weight: 400;">GitHub</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;">, </span><a target="_blank" href="https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-cloud/blog/mobility/automotive/2026/10/21/advancing-autonomous-vehicle-development-with-microsoft-nvidia-porsche-and-voxel51/"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Microsoft Azure</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;">, </span><a target="_blank" href="https://nebius.com/blog/posts/nebius-expands-physical-ai-workbench-agentic-workflows-nvidia-cosmos%E2%81%A0%EF%BF%BD” with “https://nebius.com/blog/posts/physical-ai-workbench"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Nebius</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;"> and </span><a target="_blank" href="https://coreweave.com/news/coreweave-unveils-coreweave-physical-ai-launchables-taking-the-setup-work-out-of-robotics-and-av-development"><span style="font-weight: 400;">CoreWeave</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;">:</span></p>
<ul>
<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><b>Autonomous Vehicle Dataset Generation:</b><span style="font-weight: 400;"> Curates and labels driving data, then adds synthetic examples of hard-to-capture scenarios to expand training coverage.</span></li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><b>Image Attribute Augmentation:</b><span style="font-weight: 400;"> Varies object appearance to improve image search.</span></li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><b>Event Video Generation:</b><span style="font-weight: 400;"> Turns still images into synthetic videos of rare or unsafe events.</span></li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><b>Defect Image Generation:</b><span style="font-weight: 400;"> Generates printed circuit board, metal and glass defect images for inspection models.</span></li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><b>Video Data Augmentation:</b><span style="font-weight: 400;"> Changes weather and lighting in existing footage while preserving scene structure.</span></li>
</ul>

<p><em><span style="font-weight: 400;">Cosmos Data Factory </span><span style="font-weight: 400;">accelerates </span><span style="font-weight: 400;">data curation, processing and synthetic data generation with agentic skills.</span></em></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Companies are already putting Cosmos Data Factory blueprints to work to speed their physical AI development workflows. </span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Porsche</span><span style="font-weight: 400;"> is leading the development of a shared AV data flywheel across </span><span style="font-weight: 400;">Volkswagen Group </span><span style="font-weight: 400;">brands, using Cosmos Data Factory through </span><span style="font-weight: 400;">Voxel51</span><span style="font-weight: 400;"> on </span><span style="font-weight: 400;">Microsoft Azure. </span><span style="font-weight: 400;">This is expected to reduce repeated physical test drives by up to 90% and shorten development cycle times by up to 85%.</span></p>

<p><a target="_blank" href="https://www.ultralytics.com/blog/nvidia-cosmos-yolo-synthetic-defect-detection?utm_source=nvidia&amp;utm_medium=referra"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Ultralytics</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;">, a computer vision company, retrained its YOLO model with real images and targeted synthetic defects generated using Cosmos Data Factory, improving defect localization and recall.</span></p>
<h2><b>Train, Adapt and Optimize Models</b></h2>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">With training data prepared, developers may train their own architecture or start with a pretrained model.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">At GTC Berlin, NVIDIA extended</span> <a target="_blank" href="https://github.com/NVIDIA/cosmos-framework"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Cosmos Framework</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;"> training, optimization and deployment tools and recipes as agent skills to more open models, along with Cosmos 3, giving world model builders a faster path from experimentation to deployment.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Developers and agents can now adapt open models like <a target="_blank" href="https://developer.nvidia.com/isaac/gr00t">GR00T</a>, <a target="_blank" href="https://www.nvidia.com/en-us/solutions/autonomous-vehicles/alpamayo/">Alpamayo</a>, QWEN, Pi0.5 and other compatible </span><span style="font-weight: 400;">Hugging Face</span><span style="font-weight: 400;"> models through fine-tuning and reinforcement learning, optimize the model with distillation and quantization, and deploy with high-performing inference.<br />
</span></p>
<figure id="attachment_99133" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-99133" style="width: 960px" class="wp-caption alignnone"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="wp-image-99133 size-medium" src="https://blogs.nvidia.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/Cosmos-Framework-960x538.png" alt="Diagram of a model builder agent using skills and open model backbones to prepare data, train, fine-tune, apply reinforcement learning, and optimize world models for inference. <p><span style="font-weight: 400;">To develop a world model specialized for egocentric manipulation, robotics research lab </span><span style="font-weight: 400;">MicroAGI</span><span style="font-weight: 400;"> post-trained Cosmos 3 on videos of humans manipulating objects and distilled its 32-billion-parameter reasoner down to an 8-billion-parameter Nano model with no loss in accuracy. </span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Developers can label data and post-train Cosmos 3 on</span> <a target="_blank" href="http://blog.roboflow.com/roboflow-joins-nvidia-cosmos-coalition"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Roboflow</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;"> for their specific environments and tasks, then deploy those models to </span><span style="font-weight: 400;">RoboFlow</span><span style="font-weight: 400;"> hosted or on-premises NVIDIA GPUs to bring video reasoning into factories and warehouses.</span></p>
<h2><b>Test Policies and Run Models Faster </b></h2>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Alongside these tools, NVIDIA continues advancing foundational technology through its work on Cosmos models.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">New </span><a target="_blank" href="https://huggingface.co/collections/nvidia/cosmos3-examples"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Cosmos 3 Nano model variants</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;"> for simulation enable developers to test autonomous policies in repeatable, hard-to-capture, multiview multisensor scenarios. Accompanying code allows developers to adapt the models to train and evaluate their own policies for autonomous vehicles and bimanual robots in interactive simulations before real-world testing.</span></p>
<figure id="attachment_99134" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-99134" style="width: 1280px" class="wp-caption alignnone"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="wp-image-99134 size-full" src="https://blogs.nvidia.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/Cosmos-Video-Generation.gif" alt="Cosmos creates videos using a starting image from each camera, a short description of each view, WSM controls and one LiDAR scan of the surroundings." width="1280" height="720" /><figcaption id="caption-attachment-99134" class="wp-caption-text">Multiview and multisensory video data generation for evaluating policies in closed loop simulation.</figcaption></figure>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Developers can deploy core Cosmos models using updated </span><a target="_blank" href="https://catalog.ngc.nvidia.com/orgs/nim/nvidia/containers/cosmos3/-"><span style="font-weight: 400;">NVIDIA Cosmos 3 NIM</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;"> microservices, which deliver up to 2x faster inference with no quality loss in validated configurations, while continuous video analysis enables sub-second alerts. </span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">For vision AI developers, the new </span><a target="_blank" href="https://build.nvidia.com/nvidia/video-search-and-summarization"><span style="font-weight: 400;">NVIDIA Metropolis Blueprint for video search and summarization (VSS) 3.3</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;">, built on Cosmos NIM microservices, turns one prompt into an efficient video AI application and helps teams monitor 40% more video streams with the same GPU. </span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">ADNOC</span><span style="font-weight: 400;"> uses </span><span style="font-weight: 400;">Fogsphere’s MistIQ agentic platform</span><span style="font-weight: 400;">,</span><span style="font-weight: 400;"> built with Cosmos and the </span><span style="font-weight: 400;">NVIDIA Metropolis VSS Blueprint</span><span style="font-weight: 400;">, to detect safety hazards in real time across 65 sites. For rare events, </span><a target="_blank" href="https://fogsphere.com/fogsphere-scales-ai-agents-at-adnoc/"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Fogsphere</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;"> also uses the Cosmos Data Factory to generate synthetic training data, improving detection without needing to physically stage dangerous situations.</span></p>

										<content:encoded><![CDATA[<div id="bsf_rt_marker"></div><p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Companies around the world are building on the </span><a target="_blank" href="https://www.nvidia.com/en-us/data-center/products/dsx/"><span style="font-weight: 400;">NVIDIA DSX AI Factory Platform</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;"> to plan, simulate and run the grid and the AI factory as one system, from the substation to the rack. Their adoption of the platform responds to an acute problem.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Power is now the biggest bottleneck in infrastructure. More than $300 billion in equipment is backlogged, and over 200 gigawatts of projects are waiting in U.S. interconnection queues. </span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">One root cause of the bottleneck is siloed systems. The grid, the facility and the compute inside have long been designed independently from one another, creating the conditions for delays, risk and stranded capacity.</span></p>
<div style="margin: 0 !important; padding: 0 !important; text-align: center; line-height: 1;"><a style="display: inline-flex; align-items: center; justify-content: center; margin: 0 !important; padding: 10px !important; background-color: #76b900; color: #ffffff !important; font-family: 'NVIDIA Sans', Arial, sans-serif; font-size: 16px; font-weight: bold; line-height: 1 !important; text-align: center; text-decoration: none !important; white-space: nowrap; vertical-align: top;" href="https://blogs.nvidia.com/gtc-berlin-2026-news/">Read All NVIDIA in Europe Announcements</a></div>
<p>&nbsp;</p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">To help address this, NVIDIA DSX brings these systems together:</span></p>
<ul>
<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><b>NVIDIA DSX Sim and SimReady assets</b><span style="font-weight: 400;"> from leading energy manufacturers let engineers simulate the grid, substation and AI factory together, validating generators, switchgear, power systems and cooling before breaking ground.</span></li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><a target="_blank" href="https://www.nvidia.com/en-us/data-center/products/dsx/flex/"><b>NVIDIA </b><b>DSX Flex</b></a><span style="font-weight: 400;"> syncs AI factory demand with grid conditions and onsite generation, turning AI factories from static loads into flexible grid assets.</span></li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><b>NVIDIA DSX MaxLPS and DSX Exchange</b><span style="font-weight: 400;"> squeeze more compute from every watt and securely share signals across compute, networking, energy, cooling and operations.</span></li>
</ul>
<p><b>GE Vernova</b><span style="font-weight: 400;"> is deploying its grid-to-facility and rack power expertise to complement NVIDIA DSX systems, enabling customers to transform power constraints into competitive advantages while maximizing compute and token output, sustainability and grid reliability. </span></p>
<p><b>Hitachi Energy</b><span style="font-weight: 400;">’s battery energy storage system has qualified as </span><a target="_blank" href="https://www.nvidia.com/en-us/data-center/products/dsx/dsx-ready/"><span style="font-weight: 400;">NVIDIA DSX Ready</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;">,  helping customers identify power infrastructure that meets applicable NVIDIA DSX reference architecture design requirements for resilient, reliable and scalable AI factories.</span></p>
<p><b>Schneider Electric</b><span style="font-weight: 400;">, together with AVEVA and ETAP, is advancing NVIDIA DSX with validated </span><a target="_blank" href="https://www.nvidia.com/en-us/data-center/technologies/rubin/"><span style="font-weight: 400;">NVIDIA Vera Rubin</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;"> power and cooling reference architectures and an </span><a target="_blank" href="https://www.nvidia.com/en-us/omniverse/"><span style="font-weight: 400;">NVIDIA Omniverse</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;">-powered AI factory digital twin. </span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Combining </span><b>AVEVA</b><span style="font-weight: 400;">’s engineering software expertise with NVIDIA SimReady assets, OpenUSD and Omniverse enables connecting simulations, real-time operational data and AI-driven optimization across AVEVA, Schneider Electric and third-party solutions. Together, these capabilities help organizations design, simulate, operate and optimize data centers across their lifecycle, improving energy efficiency and workload performance while accelerating time to token. </span></p>
<p><b>Siemens Energy</b><span style="font-weight: 400;"> is expanding its collaboration with NVIDIA by developing a reference design for resilient, scalable and grid-aware power infrastructure across power generation, transmission and distribution. This design is aligned with the requirements of the NVIDIA DSX Vera Rubin AI factory design. The company is also exploring usingNVIDIA technologies such as RAPIDS, Metropolis and Isaac Sim for its Noedra digital twin platform, evaluating how the technologies could enhance grid infrastructure monitoring, identify emerging risks and support unplanned outage prevention.</span></p>
<p><b>Phaidra</b><span style="font-weight: 400;"> integrated DSX Flex, DSX Max-Q and DSX Exchange into its self-learning agents, optimizing cooling and power allocation to maximize tokens per watt, improve grid flexibility and deliver about 10% more compute within the same power envelope.</span></p>
<p><b>Emerald AI</b><span style="font-weight: 400;"> integrated its Conductor platform into DSX Flex to help utilities tap underused grid capacity, an approach modeled to unlock up to 100 gigawatts on the existing U.S. grid.</span></p>

										<content:encoded><![CDATA[<div id="bsf_rt_marker"></div><p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Weather forecasts shape more than daily plans; timely weather information is crucial for energy producers, insurers and transport operators to plan around changing conditions. A global forecast can show a storm taking shape, but decisions about solar farms, shipping routes or insured property depend on what happens locally.</span></p>
<p><a target="_blank" href="https://www.nvidia.com/en-us/high-performance-computing/earth-2/"><span style="font-weight: 400;">NVIDIA Earth-2</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;"> gives developers and researchers a family of open AI models and tools they can customize with their own data. Running on NVIDIA computing, these technologies support global weather prediction, detailed regional forecasts and environmental research.</span></p>
<div style="margin: 0 !important; padding: 0 !important; text-align: center; line-height: 1;"><a style="display: inline-flex; align-items: center; justify-content: center; margin: 0 !important; padding: 10px !important; background-color: #76b900; color: #ffffff !important; font-family: 'NVIDIA Sans', Arial, sans-serif; font-size: 16px; font-weight: bold; line-height: 1 !important; text-align: center; text-decoration: none !important; white-space: nowrap; vertical-align: top;" href="https://blogs.nvidia.com/gtc-berlin-2026-news/">Read All NVIDIA in Europe Announcements</a></div>

<p><b>StormScope EU</b><span style="font-weight: 400;"> is the latest model to join Earth-2 — a regional version of the StormScope model for short-term forecasting, trained over Europe weather and climate data. It uses EUMETSAT satellite observations to predict evolving cloud conditions across 16 Meteosat channels at 10-minute intervals.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">At GTC Berlin, NVIDIA is showcasing a live StormScope EU pipeline in an interactive weather demo built in partnership with </span><a target="_blank" href="https://www.earthmover.io/blog/building-a-low-latency-earth-2-nowcasting-pipeline-with-earthmover"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Earthmover</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;">. The demo uses Earth2Studio to run forecasts and </span><span style="font-weight: 400;">Earthmover’s</span><span style="font-weight: 400;"> data platform to manage and serve the real-time results, which are updated as soon as new satellite observations come in, every 10 minutes. Earthmover serves these results as map tiles, letting visitors explore predictions across Europe without downloading entire datasets.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Developers can combine Earth-2 models in workflows tailored to regional needs, using StormScope for short-term forecasting and CorrDiff to add local detail to coarser weather data. Developers can also pick up these model architectures and train them for new environmental applications such as air-quality modeling. The open source </span><a target="_blank" href="https://developer.nvidia.com/physicsnemo"><span style="font-weight: 400;">NVIDIA PhysicsNeMo</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;"> framework provides training and customization tools so teams can build applications that incorporate proprietary data and domain expertise to meet regional needs.</span></p>

										<content:encoded><![CDATA[<div id="bsf_rt_marker"></div><p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Governments need modern solutions to serve the public while keeping sensitive information under their control.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Open models provide a path forward, letting governments evaluate them, adapt them to local languages and public services, run them on infrastructure they control, apply their own safeguards and keep sensitive data within controlled environments. </span><span style="font-weight: 400;">Some open models go further, with the datasets they were trained on published so governments can verify if training data was licensed, biased or compliant.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">NVIDIA partners and European governments are teaming to bring  advanced models to boost the efficiency of public administration while retaining control of data and infrastructure. </span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Several are also building editions of the </span><a target="_blank" href="https://huggingface.co/collections/nvidia/nemotron-personas"><span style="font-weight: 400;">NVIDIA Nemotron-Personas collection</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;">, the partner-grounded datasets behind </span><a target="_blank" href="https://github.com/NVIDIA-NeMo/UserSim"><span style="font-weight: 400;">NeMo User Sim</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;">, NVIDIA’s open source library for population-scale user simulation that dynamically evaluates and trains models and agents in local languages.</span></p>
<div style="margin: 0 !important; padding: 0 !important; text-align: center; line-height: 1;"><a style="display: inline-flex; align-items: center; justify-content: center; margin: 0 !important; padding: 10px !important; background-color: #76b900; color: #ffffff !important; font-family: 'NVIDIA Sans', Arial, sans-serif; font-size: 16px; font-weight: bold; line-height: 1 !important; text-align: center; text-decoration: none !important; white-space: nowrap; vertical-align: top;" href="https://blogs.nvidia.com/gtc-berlin-2026-news/">Read All NVIDIA in Europe Announcements</a></div>
<p>&nbsp;</p>
<h2><b>European Commission: Scaling Governed AI Applications</b></h2>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Berlin-based software company deepset is expanding its </span><a target="_blank" href="https://nam11.safelinks.protection.outlook.com/?url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.deepset.ai%2Fhaystack-platform&amp;data=05%7C02%7Cavikanova%40nvidia.com%7Cb1aac5c4554e4102010a08df2b03cf00%7C43083d15727340c1b7db39efd9ccc17a%7C0%7C0%7C639276967559950354%7CUnknown%7CTWFpbGZsb3d8eyJFbXB0eU1hcGkiOnRydWUsIlYiOiIwLjAuMDAwMCIsIlAiOiJXaW4zMiIsIkFOIjoiTWFpbCIsIldUIjoyfQ%3D%3D%7C0%7C%7C%7C&amp;sdata=2ee0ZpSWJYDNkZL0P1b5YrKYwTB4CKcKtgIATGoZgtc%3D&amp;reserved=0"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Haystack Enterprise Platform</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;">, the foundation of AI@EC, the </span><a target="_blank" href="https://www.deepset.ai/case-studies/european-commission-deepset"><span style="font-weight: 400;">European Commission’s shared platform</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;"> for building and running AI applications on commission-managed infrastructure.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">The update connects agent development, execution, optimization and governance for all customers, adding capabilities to compare operating costs, evaluate answer quality and trace problems. </span></p>
<p><a target="_blank" href="https://docs.nvidia.com/deeplearning/triton-inference-server/user-guide/docs/index.html"><span style="font-weight: 400;">NVIDIA Triton Inference Server</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;"> supports the shared services used to find and rank relevant information. The expansion builds on operating requirements demonstrated by deployments such as AI@EC, where reusable components, role-based access, audit trails and guardrails support applications including document summarization, knowledge management and multilingual policy analysis.</span></p>
<h2><b>Poland: Advanced Computing for Healthcare and Employment</b></h2>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Deloitte is helping Poland build agentic systems for healthcare and employment services using open models. </span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Poland’s eHealth Centre is building its Intelligent Services Platform for medical imaging with </span><a target="_blank" href="https://www.nvidia.com/en-us/data-center/dgx-basepod/"><span style="font-weight: 400;">NVIDIA DGX BasePOD</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;"> and the MONAI framework. The tool will help physicians analyze X-rays, CT scans and MRI images, detect abnormalities and prioritize urgent cases, reducing the time patients wait for results.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">With Deloitte’s support, Poland’s Ministry of Family, Labour and Social Policy is developing an agentic factory on NVIDIA accelerated computing infrastructure. Designed to support more than 400 labor offices, it will run models including PLLuM, GPT OSS and Inkling for document search, summarization and digital assistants, helping staff more efficiently assist job seekers.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">NVIDIA is also working with SpeakLeash in Poland and the University of Ljubljana in Slovenia on country-specific additions to its synthetic personas program, with localized datasets planned to follow.</span></p>
<h2><b>UK: Data for Locally Relevant Advanced Modeling</b></h2>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">London-based </span><a target="_blank" href="https://www.locailabs.com/blog/nvidia-nemotron-personas-uk-technical-report"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Locai Labs</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;"> released </span><a target="_blank" href="https://huggingface.co/datasets/nvidia/Nemotron-Personas-UK"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Nemotron-Personas-UK</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;">, a dataset of 1 million synthetic personas reflecting the U.K.’s demographic and geographic diversity while reducing privacy risk. Potential uses include healthcare evaluation and bias testing across age groups, education levels and rural and urban populations. For developers building public-service models, it offers a way to test whether models work consistently across different communities.</span></p>
<h2><b>Germany: Digital Assistants and AI for Classified Work</b></h2>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">In Germany, <a target="_blank" href="http://google.com/url?q=https://www.pressebox.de/pressemitteilung/materna-information-communications-se/materna-stellt-sinfonie-vor-eine-on-premises-plattform-fr-digital-humans/boxid/1317329&amp;sa=D&amp;source=docs&amp;ust=1792606876214470&amp;usg=AOvVaw2rmPdVSxrnuUrumXURJ1jn">Materna</a> is introducing Sinfonie, an on-premises platform combining generative AI, voice interaction and 3D avatars, with </span><a target="_blank" href="https://developer.nvidia.com/blog/nvidia-open-sources-audio2face-animation-model/"><span style="font-weight: 400;">NVIDIA Audio2Face</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;"> available for animation. Built on a project for a German state administration, it helps employees access institutional knowledge through spoken questions while keeping data on their organization’s infrastructure.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">For organizations handling classified information, secunet, NVIDIA, <a target="_blank" href="https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20261021042665/en/Red-Hat-AI-Factory-with-NVIDIA-Accelerates-Agentic-AI-Security-Capabilities-Across-the-Open-Hybrid-Cloud">Red Hat</a> and <a target="_blank" href="https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/deepset-unveils-reference-architecture-for-self-improving-sovereign-ai-agents-on-nvidia-infrastructure-302914982.html">deepset</a> are going a step further. The four companies are developing a sovereign cloud offering built on secunet’s proven SINA Cloud foundation, which includes security components that have been authorized for use with classified information. The planned offering combines four components: NVIDIA accelerated computing and </span><a target="_blank" href="https://www.nvidia.com/en-us/ai-data-science/foundation-models/nemotron/"><span style="font-weight: 400;">NVIDIA Nemotron</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;"> open models; Red Hat OpenShift AI for deploying and managing AI workloads; deepset’s Haystack AI agent orchestration; and infrastructure operated by secunet in German data centers. </span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">To support local development, <a target="_blank" href="https://www.iis.fraunhofer.de/en/ff/amm/allinga/ai-model-factory/soofi.html">the Sovereign Open Source Foundation Models research initiative</a> (Soofi) is developing a family of AI models for German industry and the public sector. That includes </span><a target="_blank" href="https://huggingface.co/datasets/nvidia/Nemotron-Personas-Germany"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Nemotron-Personas-Germany,</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;"> an open dataset of 1 million records, each with six synthetic personas, grounded in the 2022 census across all 16 federal states and 400 districts. Created with </span><a target="_blank" href="https://docs.nvidia.com/nemo/datadesigner/getting-started/welcome"><span style="font-weight: 400;">NVIDIA NeMo Data Designer</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;"> without real people’s private data, the dataset helps developers test whether public-service models work consistently across regions, age groups and communities. Like the U.K. edition, it can be used with </span><a target="_blank" href="https://github.com/NVIDIA-NeMo/UserSim/"><span style="font-weight: 400;">NeMo User Sim</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;"> so developers can evaluate and train models against simulated users based on synthetic German personas.</span></p>
<h2><b>France: Advanced Open Models in the Public Sector Workspace</b></h2>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">CANUT, France’s public purchasing body for digital services, selected </span><a target="_blank" href="https://linagora.com/sites/default/files/2026-10/PR_LINAGORA_CANUT_0.pdf"><span style="font-weight: 400;">LINAGORA</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;"> and Docaposte to provide a sovereign collaborative workspace built on Twake.ai. </span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">More than 2,500 eligible organizations, including municipalities, schools and healthcare institutions, can access the framework without running their own tender. The workspace offers public employees email, document sharing and collaboration tools, with NVIDIA technology supporting speech transcription and infrastructure hosted in France. Région Occitanie is the preferred partner for scaling deployment.</span></p>
<h2><b>Greece: A Local Agentic Ecosystem</b></h2>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Greek AI and energy company </span><a target="_blank" href="https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/from-energy-tokens-european-ai-ecosystem-showcased-nvidia-gtc-ystxe/"><span style="font-weight: 400;">KIEFER</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;"> is developing an agentic factory built on NVIDIA DGX SuperPOD with DGX GB200 alongside Sophea open weight models and Greek-language tools for knowledge retrieval, speech and digital assistants. These capabilities stand to make institutional information easier to find and use for Greek-speaking public sector teams, while giving organizations control over where agents run and how data is handled.</span></p>
<h2><b>Codefest: Building the Next Applications</b></h2>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">In an effort to support public sector organizations in integrating agentic AI in Europe and beyond, NVIDIA Open Models Codefest brings together startups, software providers and labs across the region to turn open models into locally controlled solutions for public sector challenges, including medical reasoning, AI tutoring and model governance. The top five teams are </span><a target="_blank" href="https://pleias.ai/"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Pleias</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;">, </span><a target="_blank" href="http://zehnlab.ai"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Zehnlab.ai</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;">, </span><a target="_blank" href="https://www.disseqt.ai/"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Disseqt</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;">, </span><a target="_blank" href="https://algoritmica.ai/"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Algoritmica GmbH</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;"> and </span><a target="_blank" href="https://aba.technology/en/"><span style="font-weight: 400;">ABA Technology</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;">. <a target="_blank" href="http://google.com/url?q=https://www.t-systems.com/de/en/insights/newsroom/news/deutsche-telekom-powers-open-models-ai-codefest-across-emea-with-nvidia-1221678&amp;sa=D&amp;source=docs&amp;ust=1792594206181923&amp;usg=AOvVaw0PljEq-38oAirZa6cqYiwu">Deutsche Telekom and T-Systems</a> have provided the compute infrastructure for teams to train and deploy large language models. </span></p>

										<content:encoded><![CDATA[<div id="bsf_rt_marker"></div><p><span style="font-weight: 400;">NVIDIA cuDSS, launched today in general availability, is an </span><a target="_blank" href="https://developer.nvidia.com/cuda/cuda-x-libraries"><span style="font-weight: 400;">NVIDIA CUDA-X library</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;"> that shifts a critical part of engineering and scientific simulations onto GPUs. </span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Complex engineering feats — from advanced chips to aircraft to industrial systems —– depend on simulation to predict how designs will perform before they’re built. As designs become more complex, simulations take longer to run, increasing computing demands and limiting the number of ideas teams can test.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">NVIDIA cuDSS accelerates solving large sparse systems — the math-heavy step that repeats throughout engineering and scientific simulations and often limits how many designs a team can test — by tapping into GPUs. This lets applications reduce time to results and increase simulation throughput, enabling end users to explore larger more realistic models, more accurate representations and a wider design space at a reduced cost.</span></p>
<p>&nbsp;</p>
<div style="margin: 0 !important; padding: 0 !important; text-align: center; line-height: 1;"><a style="display: inline-flex; align-items: center; justify-content: center; margin: 0 !important; padding: 10px !important; background-color: #76b900; color: #ffffff !important; font-family: 'NVIDIA Sans', Arial, sans-serif; font-size: 16px; font-weight: bold; line-height: 1 !important; text-align: center; text-decoration: none !important; white-space: nowrap; vertical-align: top;" href="https://blogs.nvidia.com/gtc-berlin-2026-news/">Read All NVIDIA in Europe Announcements</a></div>

<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><b>COMSOL:</b><span style="font-weight: 400;"> Using COMSOL Multiphysics with NVIDIA cuDSS on NVIDIA accelerated computing, ZEISS SMT achieved a measured 4x speedup in an EUV lithography plasma simulation benchmark, completing the full simulation in six days and 14 hours.</span></li>

The portfolio includes </span><a target="_blank" href="https://huggingface.co/collections/nvidia/nvidia-kumo-structured"><span style="font-weight: 400;">NVIDIA Kumo Structured</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;">, with models for relational and tabular prediction, forecasting and anomaly detection, and </span><a target="_blank" href="https://github.com/NVIDIA/kumo-engine"><span style="font-weight: 400;">NVIDIA Kumo Engine</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;">, an open library stack for developing and deploying structured data foundation models. </span></p>
<p><a target="_blank" href="https://www.cognite.com/en/partners/nvidia"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Cognite</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;">, </span><span style="font-weight: 400;">Coinbase</span><span style="font-weight: 400;">,</span><span style="font-weight: 400;"> Databricks</span><span style="font-weight: 400;">, </span><span style="font-weight: 400;">DoorDash</span><span style="font-weight: 400;">, </span><span style="font-weight: 400;">Revolut</span><span style="font-weight: 400;"> and </span><span style="font-weight: 400;">Tubi</span><span style="font-weight: 400;"> are among the first to work with Kumo models to accelerate development. </span><a target="_blank" href="https://www.snowflake.com/en/blog/nvidia-kumo-graph-ml-snowflake/"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Snowflake </span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;">customers are already using Kumo models to accelerate AI development in industries including advertising, energy, financial services and retail.</span></p>
<h2><b>NVIDIA Cosmos Accelerates Robotics Training and Simulation</b></h2>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">NVIDIA Cosmos is a family of open world foundation models that helps developers build robots, autonomous vehicles and vision agents that understand and interact with the physical world. </span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">NVIDIA is introducing </span><a href="https://blogs.nvidia.com/blog/cosmos-open-train-evaluate-deploy-world-models"><span style="font-weight: 400;">new Cosmos post-training and optimization tools</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;"> to help developers improve world models using less compute, along with simulation checkpoints for evaluating behavior in interactive driving and robotic manipulation scenarios before real-world testing.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">NVIDIA is also introducing Cosmos Data Factory — comprising blueprints and core agent skills — to help developers curate, label, generate and evaluate training data for driving scenarios, product defects, image augmentation and event generation. Developers can use complete workflows or individual capabilities with agents coordinating the process. </span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Porsche</span><span style="font-weight: 400;"> with </span><span style="font-weight: 400;">Voxel51</span><span style="font-weight: 400;">, </span><a target="_blank" href="https://encord.com/data-labs/nvidia-anybotics-synthetic-data/"><span style="font-weight: 400;">ANYbotics</span><span style="font-weight: 400;"> with </span><span style="font-weight: 400;">Encord</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;">, </span><span style="font-weight: 400;">Zalando </span><span style="font-weight: 400;">and </span><span style="font-weight: 400;">CEVA Logistics</span><span style="font-weight: 400;"> with </span><span style="font-weight: 400;">Sereact</span><span style="font-weight: 400;">, and </span><a target="_blank" href="https://news.siemens.com/en-us/siemens-nvidia-pcba-digital-twin-factory/"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Siemens</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;"> are using Cosmos Data Factory to create high-quality, diverse training datasets and improve model precision.</span></p>
<h2><b>NVIDIA Alpamayo 2 Brings Real-Time Reasoning to Autonomous Vehicles</b></h2>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">NVIDIA Alpamayo 2 Edge is a compact reasoning model that brings real-time scene understanding and motion planning directly into autonomous vehicles. Built on NVIDIA Cosmos and optimized for the NVIDIA DRIVE AGX Thor platform, it helps developers bring advanced driving capabilities into vehicles with lower computing requirements.</span></p>
<h2><b>NVIDIA BioNeMo RFD4-Proteina Advances Protein Design </b></h2>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">NVIDIA BioNeMo RFD4-Proteina is an open protein design model from NVIDIA and the </span><span style="font-weight: 400;">Baker Lab at the University of Washington’s Institute for Protein Design.</span><span style="font-weight: 400;"> </span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">The model generates protein sequences and 3D structures to help researchers design proteins that bind to difficult targets, assemble into complex structures and bind to small molecules. </span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">NVIDIA BioNeMo RFD4-Proteina delivers leading performance across more than 10 protein design tasks and generates designs about 13x faster than RFdiffusion3 in a benchmark of 200 amino acid proteins. Researchers can use the model through agents to generate and evaluate candidates against design goals and molecular constraints, then choose which designs to test in the lab. </span></p>
<h2><b>NVIDIA Ising Accelerates Quantum Development</b></h2>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">The NVIDIA Ising family of open models provides a common, extensible foundation to speed the breakthroughs needed to develop useful quantum supercomputers, from real-time hardware calibration to error correction, across different qubit modalities and tailored to individual hardware needs. </span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">IQM Quantum Computers</span><span style="font-weight: 400;"> is using Ising to calibrate its quantum processors, showcasing how specialized open calibration models outperform large general-purpose models. </span><span style="font-weight: 400;">SEEQC</span><span style="font-weight: 400;"> is combining the NVIDIA NVQLink platform and Ising with its unique Digital Link to meet the low-latency requirements needed for real-time quantum error correction.</span></p>
<p><a target="_blank" href="https://aws.amazon.com/blogs/quantum-computing/decoding-quantum-errors-with-nvidia-ising-decoding-on-aws/"><span style="font-weight: 400;">AWS</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;"> is making NVIDIA Ising readily available to researchers by integrating the model family into AWS Parallel Computing Service (AWS PCS). While decoding is typically performed on classical hardware colocated with the quantum processor due to latency requirements, the AWS PCS integration lets researchers build on Ising prior to deployment.</span></p>

<p><span style="font-weight: 400">“This now represents the breadth of technology necessary to create the AI factory,” Huang said. “This is what NVIDIA builds today, and this is the foundation by which we can enable Europe to be part of the AI ecosystem, the AI economy.”  </span><span style="font-weight: 400"><br />
</span><span style="font-weight: 400"><br />
</span><span style="font-weight: 400">He said Vera Rubin, in full production, extends NVIDIA’s computing platform beyond models and large language models to process the entire AI agent. He also showed several of NVIDIA’s latest rack-scale hardware offerings.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400">Next, </span><span style="font-weight: 400">Huang announced </span><a target="_blank" href="https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/nvidia-introduces-dsx-pod-ai-factories-to-accelerate-europes-infrastructure-deployment"><span style="font-weight: 400">NVIDIA DSX POD</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400">, a reference design for complete NVIDIA Vera Rubin AI factories built by partners inside modular containers, integrated and validated before delivery. With DSX POD, onsite deployment can shrink from six months to just 14 days once site utilities are ready. </span></p>

<h2><b>Agents Are the New Computing Platform — and Safety Is an Engineering Problem</b></h2>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400">Huang said agents think, reason, iterate and use tools, driving token consumption as they become more useful. </span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400">He described an agent as comprising five elements: a model, a harness that orchestrates its work and memory, a sandbox that isolates the harness, knowledge and tools. In that framing, an agent is more than a model answering questions; it can draw on knowledge and act through tools.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400">“The most important thing is to make sure the agent is secure,” Huang said. The autonomous agent can access files, resources, networks and tools, so its privileges should be minimal and it should be monitored.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400">NVIDIA’s answer is the new </span><a target="_blank" href="https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/openshell-reference-architecture">NVIDIA OpenShell Reference Architecture for Safe AI Agents</a><span style="font-weight: 400">, a full-stack design built on </span><a target="_blank" href="https://www.nvidia.com/en-us/ai/openshell/"><span style="font-weight: 400">NVIDIA OpenShell</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400"> — an open source, policy-controlled runtime with kernel-level sandboxing that sits between an agent and the systems it can affect.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-99305 aligncenter" src="https://blogs.nvidia.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/jhh-keynote-openshell-1920x1080-1-1680x945.jpg" alt="" width="1200" height="675" srcset="https://blogs.nvidia.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/jhh-keynote-openshell-1920x1080-1-1680x945.jpg 1680w, https://blogs.nvidia.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/jhh-keynote-openshell-1920x1080-1-960x540.jpg 960w, https://blogs.nvidia.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/jhh-keynote-openshell-1920x1080-1-1280x720.jpg 1280w, https://blogs.nvidia.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/jhh-keynote-openshell-1920x1080-1-1536x864.jpg 1536w, https://blogs.nvidia.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/jhh-keynote-openshell-1920x1080-1-1290x725.jpg 1290w, https://blogs.nvidia.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/jhh-keynote-openshell-1920x1080-1-630x354.jpg 630w, https://blogs.nvidia.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/jhh-keynote-openshell-1920x1080-1-300x169.jpg 300w, https://blogs.nvidia.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/jhh-keynote-openshell-1920x1080-1-400x225.jpg 400w, https://blogs.nvidia.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/jhh-keynote-openshell-1920x1080-1.jpg 1920w" sizes="auto, (max-width: 1200px) 100vw, 1200px" /> </span><span style="font-weight: 400"><br />
</span><span style="font-weight: 400">That means privileges, policy enforcement and monitoring must adapt dynamically. “Every single time an agent is launched, the privileges are different,” Huang said.</span></p>
<p><iframe loading="lazy" title="NVIDIA OpenShell Reference Architecture for Safe AI Agents | NVIDIA GTC Berlin 2026 Keynote" width="1200" height="675" src="https://www.youtube.com/embed/H6_cjcgnwHo?feature=oembed" frameborder="0" allow="accelerometer; autoplay; clipboard-write; encrypted-media; gyroscope; picture-in-picture; web-share" referrerpolicy="strict-origin-when-cross-origin" allowfullscreen></iframe></p>
<h2><b>Open Models Turn Expertise Into an Advantage</b></h2>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400">Huang </span><a href="https://blogs.nvidia.com/blog/open-models-europe-industry/"><span style="font-weight: 400">presented NVIDIA’s open models</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400"> as a starting point for building AI tailored to specific needs. </span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400">NVIDIA’s frontier, open models are available for use across industries: Nemotron for language, Cosmos for world foundation models, GR00T for humanoid robotics, Alpamayo for autonomous vehicles, Ising for quantum-classical computing and Earth-2 for physics AI. </span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400">“We are the frontier,” Huang said. “So in all of these different areas, we offer you all of the models, and we’re continuously working on it. We’ve been working on it for a decade, and we’ll continue to work on this forever.”</span></p>
<p><iframe loading="lazy" title="Open Models Turn Expertise Into an Advantage | | NVIDIA GTC Berlin 2026 Keynote" width="1200" height="675" src="https://www.youtube.com/embed/hzWwHwB7qls?feature=oembed" frameborder="0" allow="accelerometer; autoplay; clipboard-write; encrypted-media; gyroscope; picture-in-picture; web-share" referrerpolicy="strict-origin-when-cross-origin" allowfullscreen></iframe></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400">NVIDIA provides the models, data and scripts to reproduce them, and companies can post-train a model on their own data for a particular use case — turning proprietary data into what he called “sovereign intelligence.”</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400">“You could start with an open model, something that’s incredibly good like Nemotron, and with your own data, post-train it for your use case and achieve, of course, super intelligence,” Huang said. “That is a very sensible thing to do, and all the technology exists today.”</span></p>
<h2><b>NVIDIA Gives Agents a Knowledge Layer</b></h2>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400">Huang then introduced the </span><a target="_blank" href="https://developer.nvidia.com/topics/ai/agent-knowledge-foundation"><span style="font-weight: 400">NVIDIA Agent Knowledge Foundation</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400"> as a set of models and libraries for connecting agents to enterprise data, whether it’s unstructured information in documents or structured data in databases. </span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400">He highlighted Nemotron tools for parsing documents, embedding queries and reranking relevant information, along with new </span><a target="_blank" href="https://developer.nvidia.com/topics/ai/agent-knowledge-foundation/kumo"><span style="font-weight: 400">NVIDIA Kumo</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400"> models for making predictions from structured data.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400">“Ultimately, what turns these super agents, these agents into super agents, is that you give them access to your company’s knowledge,” Huang said.</span></p>
<p><iframe loading="lazy" title="NVIDIA Gives Agents a Knowledge Layer | | NVIDIA GTC Berlin 2026 Keynote" width="1200" height="675" src="https://www.youtube.com/embed/Dw0QP6Q0Hos?feature=oembed" frameborder="0" allow="accelerometer; autoplay; clipboard-write; encrypted-media; gyroscope; picture-in-picture; web-share" referrerpolicy="strict-origin-when-cross-origin" allowfullscreen></iframe></p>
<h2><b>NVIDIA CUDA-X — Accelerated Tools for Agents</b></h2>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400">Huang then described </span><a target="_blank" href="https://www.nvidia.com/en-us/technologies/cuda-x/"><span style="font-weight: 400">NVIDIA CUDA-X</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400"> as a collection of accelerated libraries used across science, engineering, research and creative work. </span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400">NVIDIA’s accelerated computing platform has more than 11 million developers building on it. </span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400">Huang said those libraries can accelerate simulators, solvers and data-processing engines — the kinds of tools agents may draw on to do useful work.</span><span style="font-weight: 400"><br />
</span><span style="font-weight: 400"><br />
</span><span style="font-weight: 400">“CUDA-X tools are what gives agents the tools of the trade,” Huang said. </span></p>
<p><iframe loading="lazy" title="NVIDIA CUDA-X — Accelerated Tools for Agents | NVIDIA GTC Berlin 2026 Keynote" width="1200" height="675" src="https://www.youtube.com/embed/vmScD9mYVEU?feature=oembed" frameborder="0" allow="accelerometer; autoplay; clipboard-write; encrypted-media; gyroscope; picture-in-picture; web-share" referrerpolicy="strict-origin-when-cross-origin" allowfullscreen></iframe></p>
<h2><b>Agents Go to Work in Labs, Factories and Engineering</b></h2>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400">A video showcased </span><a href="https://blogs.nvidia.com/blog/enterprise-platforms-agent-toolkit/"><span style="font-weight: 400">NVIDIA Agent Toolkit</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400"> bringing company knowledge, models and tools together in three industry examples: </span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400">At Lila Sciences, agents help design a potential B-cell lymphoma therapy, using virtual experiments to guide work in an automated lab. </span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400">At Siemens’ Erlangen factory, agents help prepare new products for production, testing plans in a digital twin before changes reach the factory floor. </span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400">And at Dassault Systèmes, engineering agents adapt vehicle design, using simulation to identify a problem and refine the design.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400">“And [all this] knowledge and tools belong to you,” Huang said after the video rolled. “That’s your expertise, and so all of this is now possible to do.” </span></p>
<p><iframe loading="lazy" title="Agents Go to Work in Labs, Factories and Engineering | | NVIDIA GTC Berlin 2026 Keynote" width="1200" height="675" src="https://www.youtube.com/embed/Mbr0zH5OBM0?feature=oembed" frameborder="0" allow="accelerometer; autoplay; clipboard-write; encrypted-media; gyroscope; picture-in-picture; web-share" referrerpolicy="strict-origin-when-cross-origin" allowfullscreen></iframe></p>
<h2><b>Telcos as Distributed AI Infrastructure</b></h2>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400">Huang said AI is transforming telecommunications, improving spectrum efficiency and turning cell towers into distributed AI infrastructure. </span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400">He cited 100,000 sites and about 100 gigawatts of distributed data centers, and said </span><a target="_blank" href="https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/nvidia-nokia-and-leading-telecom-operators-turn-networks-into-distributed-intelligence-platforms"><span style="font-weight: 400">NVIDIA has made its first commercial phone call on T-Mobile’s network with Nokia</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400"> after nearly eight years of work.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400">“Now CUDA makes phone calls,” Huang said. “AI will revolutionize the way we do telecommunications. It will enhance our spectrum efficiency tremendously.” </span><span style="font-weight: 400"><br />
</span></p>
<p><iframe loading="lazy" title="Telcos as Distributed AI Infrastructure | NVIDIA GTC Berlin 2026 Keynote" width="1200" height="675" src="https://www.youtube.com/embed/duQhkyiOHA8?feature=oembed" frameborder="0" allow="accelerometer; autoplay; clipboard-write; encrypted-media; gyroscope; picture-in-picture; web-share" referrerpolicy="strict-origin-when-cross-origin" allowfullscreen></iframe></p>
<h2><b>AI Moves Into the Treatment Room</b></h2>
<p><a target="_blank" href="https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/healthcare-and-life-sciences-leaders-build-on-nvidia-to-advance-drug-discovery-and-expand-patient-care"><span style="font-weight: 400">Healthcare and life sciences leaders are building on NVIDIA technologies</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400"> to advance drug discovery and expand patient care.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400">For example, NVIDIA and Siemens Healthineers are developing AI-enabled radiation therapy. </span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400">Nemotron agents coordinate treatment planning, the Isaac for Healthcare platform and Omniverse libraries create a digital twin of the device and patient anatomy to simulate tumor targeting. Holoscan with IGX Thor connects live imaging and sensor data to enable real-time adaptive delivery as the patient moves.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400">Huang described adaptive radiotherapy as a physical-AI application: as the patient’s body and tumor change during treatment, the system must track the changes and adjust the radiation targeting.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400">“The tumor is changing in shape. The physical body is changing while you’re in therapy,” Huang said. “The robot, this radio adaptive radiotherapy system, has to perceive the changing environment, understand where the tumor is, segment it just perfectly and direct the radiotherapy accordingly.” </span></p>
<h2><b>The Road Ahead: Mobility Leaders Join NVIDIA’s Automotive Ecosystem</b><b><br />
</b></h2>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400">Huang said the </span><a target="_blank" href="https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/european-mobility-leaders-build-safe-vehicles-and-robotaxi-fleets-on-nvidia-hyperion"><span style="font-weight: 400">NVIDIA Hyperion computing platform is being designed into more vehicle fleets</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400">, with autonomous-driving software connecting robotaxis to ride-hailing services. </span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400">Bolt with Lucid, Mercedes-Benz, Volkswagen Commercial Vehicles and Einride are showcasing selected autonomous-mobility use cases on the NVIDIA Hyperion computing platform, demonstrating potential applications for level 3 and level 4 vehicles as well as future robotaxi fleets.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400">In addition, Volvo Cars is adopting Hyperion and NVIDIA Alpamayo Stack software for safe automated driving. NVIDIA also introduced new AI and safety technologies to help developers build, validate and deploy autonomous vehicles at scale.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400">Volkswagen’s van, JLR, Volvo and Mercedes-Benz’s next-generation vehicles will be Hyperion-compatible, Huang announced, saying, “Welcome to Hyperion.”</span></p>
<p><iframe loading="lazy" title="Mobility Leaders Join NVIDIA’s Automotive Ecosystem | NVIDIA GTC Berlin 2026 Keynote" width="1200" height="675" src="https://www.youtube.com/embed/c-7J5IWV9rg?feature=oembed" frameborder="0" allow="accelerometer; autoplay; clipboard-write; encrypted-media; gyroscope; picture-in-picture; web-share" referrerpolicy="strict-origin-when-cross-origin" allowfullscreen></iframe></p>
<h2><b>A Reference Humanoid for Robotics Researchers</b></h2>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400">Huang then </span><a target="_blank" href="https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/nvidia-advances-isaac-platform-for-general-purpose-robotics-with-new-reference-humanoid-frameworks-simulation-pipelines-and-data"><span style="font-weight: 400">announced Agile ONE SN</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400">, a reference humanoid built on Isaac GR00T — a 31-degree-of-freedom body, expected to be available next year, designed to cut researcher setup time from weeks to days. </span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400">The platform is modular: researchers can use the full workflow or pull in selected components. </span><span style="font-weight: 400"><br />
</span><span style="font-weight: 400">“This is a great starting point,” Huang said. </span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400">It builds on NVIDIA’s open physical AI stack — Isaac Capture for data collection, GR00T models for robot intelligence, Isaac Lab for simulation-based training, Isaac OS for runtime — and NVIDIA’s open physical AI dataset. Partners including Agility Robotics, Figure, KUKA and NEURA Robotics are already building on the same platform.</span></p>
<p><iframe loading="lazy" title="Physical AI is the Next Wave of AI | NVIDIA GTC Berlin 2026 Keynote" width="1200" height="675" src="https://www.youtube.com/embed/wrKAZw2PN6I?feature=oembed" frameborder="0" allow="accelerometer; autoplay; clipboard-write; encrypted-media; gyroscope; picture-in-picture; web-share" referrerpolicy="strict-origin-when-cross-origin" allowfullscreen></iframe></p>
<h2><b>Personal AI Gets a New Machine</b></h2>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400">Huang closed by discussing </span><a target="_blank" href="https://www.nvidia.com/en-us/products/rtx-spark/"><span style="font-weight: 400">RTX Spark laptops</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400">, which </span><a target="_blank" href="https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/nvidia-rtx-spark-pcs-available-today-a-new-beginning-for-windows-pcs"><span style="font-weight: 400">launched last week</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400">, bringing together RTX graphics and NVIDIA’s full-stack AI platform in slim, premium Windows PCs for accelerated creation, immersive gaming and powerful local AI. </span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-99319 aligncenter" src="https://blogs.nvidia.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/jhh-keynote-rtx-spark-1920x1080-1-1680x945.jpg" alt="" width="1200" height="675" srcset="https://blogs.nvidia.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/jhh-keynote-rtx-spark-1920x1080-1-1680x945.jpg 1680w, https://blogs.nvidia.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/jhh-keynote-rtx-spark-1920x1080-1-960x540.jpg 960w, https://blogs.nvidia.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/jhh-keynote-rtx-spark-1920x1080-1-1280x720.jpg 1280w, https://blogs.nvidia.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/jhh-keynote-rtx-spark-1920x1080-1-1536x864.jpg 1536w, https://blogs.nvidia.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/jhh-keynote-rtx-spark-1920x1080-1-1290x725.jpg 1290w, https://blogs.nvidia.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/jhh-keynote-rtx-spark-1920x1080-1-630x354.jpg 630w, https://blogs.nvidia.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/jhh-keynote-rtx-spark-1920x1080-1-300x169.jpg 300w, https://blogs.nvidia.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/jhh-keynote-rtx-spark-1920x1080-1-400x225.jpg 400w, https://blogs.nvidia.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/jhh-keynote-rtx-spark-1920x1080-1.jpg 1920w" sizes="auto, (max-width: 1200px) 100vw, 1200px" /></span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400">He compared its 1 petaflop of performance and 128GB of memory with the DGX-1 he delivered to OpenAI in 2016 — then made the difference in size the punchline:</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400">“In the year 2016, I delivered 1 petaflop and 128GB … to a small company called OpenAI,” Huang said, holding up the compact machine in front of the audience. “This is 1 petaflop and 128GB. The only difference, of course, is that I can hold this like this, and DGX-1 took two people to carry upstairs.”</span></p>
<p><iframe loading="lazy" title="Personal AI Gets a New Machine | NVIDIA GTC Berlin 2026 Keynote" width="1200" height="675" src="https://www.youtube.com/embed/hb6If37sSGQ?feature=oembed" frameborder="0" allow="accelerometer; autoplay; clipboard-write; encrypted-media; gyroscope; picture-in-picture; web-share" referrerpolicy="strict-origin-when-cross-origin" allowfullscreen></iframe></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400">Huang then showed a video of an agent helping train Hugging Face’s Microduck: it set up a course and simulated the robot in Isaac Sim, sent training jobs to the cloud, then brought the learned policy back to RTX Spark for evaluation before deploying it to the physical robot.</span></p>
<p><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="alignnone size-full wp-image-99333" src="https://blogs.nvidia.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/KeynotePhoto-718-scaled.jpeg" alt="" width="2048" height="1152" srcset="https://blogs.nvidia.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/KeynotePhoto-718-scaled.jpeg 2048w, https://blogs.nvidia.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/KeynotePhoto-718-960x540.jpeg 960w, https://blogs.nvidia.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/KeynotePhoto-718-1680x945.jpeg 1680w, https://blogs.nvidia.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/KeynotePhoto-718-1280x720.jpeg 1280w, https://blogs.nvidia.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/KeynotePhoto-718-1536x864.jpeg 1536w, https://blogs.nvidia.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/KeynotePhoto-718-1290x725.jpeg 1290w, https://blogs.nvidia.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/KeynotePhoto-718-630x354.jpeg 630w, https://blogs.nvidia.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/KeynotePhoto-718-300x169.jpeg 300w, https://blogs.nvidia.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/KeynotePhoto-718-400x225.jpeg 400w" sizes="auto, (max-width: 2048px) 100vw, 2048px" /></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400">A flock of four Microducks joined Huang onstage, sitting down, standing up and even taking a nap on cue. He explained that they were created on RTX Spark, trained in the cloud and learned their skills in simulation.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400">“You were created on RTX Spark … [we] trained you in the cloud on a whole bunch of NVIDIA supercomputers, and you learned your skills right here on RTX Spark and the digital twin,” Huang told them.</span></p>
<p><iframe loading="lazy" title="RTX Spark Brings Microducks to Life | NVIDIA GTC Berlin 2026 Keynote" width="1200" height="675" src="https://www.youtube.com/embed/zHBrUVyQJWc?feature=oembed" frameborder="0" allow="accelerometer; autoplay; clipboard-write; encrypted-media; gyroscope; picture-in-picture; web-share" referrerpolicy="strict-origin-when-cross-origin" allowfullscreen></iframe></p>
<h2><b>The Full Lineup</b></h2>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400">With the Microducks gathered around him, Huang recapped the keynote: Europe is building its AI economy, AI factories are evolving to run agents and physical AI is the next frontier.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400">“So that’s it,” Huang said. “Everybody can build agents. Every company in Europe will become an AI company … and we’re going to have a thriving AI economy here in Europe.”</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400">To close, Huang had one more surprise: a <a target="_blank" href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qdUQJArUk30&amp;list=RDqdUQJArUk30&amp;start_radio=1">Eurovision-style musical send-off</a> that turned the keynote’s themes into a comic recap. </span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400">The lyrics raced from OpenShell’s guardrails — “No credentials. Access dismissed” and “Sentry on BlueField never clocks off” — to physical AI, where robot training was likened to “college for robots without student loans.” </span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400">The punchline: “You can’t spell </span><a href="https://blogs.nvidia.com/blog/productive-durable-fungible-ai-factories/"><span style="font-weight: 400">fungible</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400"> without fun.” A saxophone solo led into a rousing “We are Europa” chorus.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400">“Let’s work together to build Europe’s AI economy and put AI to work,” Huang said, closing out his talk. “Have a great GTC.”</span></p>
